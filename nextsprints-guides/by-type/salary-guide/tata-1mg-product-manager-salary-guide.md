## Introduction

Tata 1mg is one of India's leading digital health companies and part of Tata Digital. Its product managers work on online pharmacy, diagnostics, teleconsultation, memberships and the systems that run delivery and labs. With core businesses EBITDA positive since December 2025 and revenue of about ₹2,936 crore in FY26, 1mg offers PMs a mix of consumer-scale problems, healthcare impact and the stability of the Tata Group.

This guide covers 2026 PM salary ranges in INR by level, how compensation is structured, how location and team affect pay, career progression and how to negotiate. All figures are estimates anchored on self-reported data from AmbitionBox and other public sources. Treat them as ranges, not guarantees.

## Tata 1mg Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, total annual compensation for PMs at Tata 1mg ranges from about ₹12 lakh for associate PMs to over ₹1 crore for directors. Public data points:

- AmbitionBox lists Tata 1mg Product Manager pay at about ₹13–47 lakh for 3–6 years of experience (32 salaries), with many employees reporting around ₹29.8 lakh fixed and about ₹1.9 lakh variable. AmbitionBox estimates this at about 46% above the average PM salary in India.
- Senior Product Manager pay is reported at about ₹30–55 lakh for 4–12 years of experience.
- Associate Product Manager pay is reported at about ₹11.4–28.5 lakh.
- Lead Product Manager pay is reported at about ₹40.5–51.8 lakh (very small sample).
- PM reviews on AmbitionBox are few, and salary and benefits ratings are mixed.

Factors influencing pay:

- Level and product experience
- Charter: diagnostics, growth and core pharmacy roles are often seen as high-impact
- Background: tier-1 MBA or strong consumer internet experience
- Competing offers
- Negotiation

Compared with the market, 1mg pays above traditional pharmacy chains and healthcare companies, roughly in line with mid-to-large Indian consumer internet companies on fixed pay, and below the top-paying startups and global tech firms once equity is included.

## Understanding Tata 1mg's PM Compensation Structure

1. Fixed Salary: The largest component, about 85–95% of total pay for most PMs Includes basic pay, allowances, provident fund and gratuity Reviewed annually
2. Variable Pay: Usually 5–15% of fixed, linked to individual and company performance AmbitionBox data suggests modest variable amounts for mid-level PMs
3. Long-Term Incentives: 1mg had ESOPs before Tata Digital's acquisition; current long-term incentive plans vary by level and are more common for senior roles Ask about any retention or long-term plans during negotiation
4. Joining Bonus: Sometimes offered to offset forfeited bonuses or unvested stock
5. Benefits: Health insurance for family; often includes health services discounts on 1mg Tata Group benefits and discounts across Tata brands Leave policies and learning programmes

### Associate/Junior PM

- 0–3 years of experience; often from engineering, analytics or campus hiring
- Works on a feature within a charter, such as search or prescription upload

### Product Manager

- 2–5 years; owns a charter end to end, for example lab booking or membership
- AmbitionBox's ₹13–47 lakh band reflects wide variation by background

### Senior PM

- 4–8 years; owns a business line or multiple squads
- Works directly with business heads on revenue and margin

### Principal PM

- Often titled Lead PM; 7–10 years; sets direction across charters
- Influences company priorities

### Director of Product

- 10+ years; leads a product portfolio and team
- Compensation can exceed ₹1 crore

- These ranges are approximate and vary by team, performance and negotiation.
- Long-term incentives, where offered, can add meaningfully for senior roles.
- Exceptional candidates may receive offers above these ranges.

## How Location Affects Tata 1mg PM Salaries

Most product roles are based in Gurugram, where 1mg has its headquarters. Pay follows the Delhi NCR market, which is competitive with Bengaluru for PM roles. A small number of roles may exist in other cities (for example, operations-linked roles near warehouses or labs), generally following similar bands.

| Location | Relative pay | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gurugram | Baseline | Most PM roles |
| Bengaluru | Similar | Few PM roles |
| Other cities | Similar or slightly lower | Operations-linked roles |

Hybrid work policies vary by team; most PMs are expected in the Gurugram office several days a week because they work closely with operations, pharmacy and lab teams.

## Negotiating Your Tata 1mg PM Offer

1. Research: use AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and conversations with current or former PMs.
2. Focus on fixed pay: it is most of the package and drives future increments.
3. Ask about variable pay history: how much was actually paid in recent years.
4. Clarify long-term incentives: whether any are offered at your level and how they vest.
5. Negotiate level and title: they shape future pay more than small fixed increases.
6. Use competing offers: offers from consumer internet or health-tech companies help.

> Negotiation Tip
> Highlight experience that maps to 1mg's priorities: retention and subscriptions, marketplace operations, logistics, healthcare compliance or diagnostics. Show how you would move a specific business metric.

## Conclusion

Tata 1mg offers PMs competitive Gurugram pay, meaningful healthcare impact and Tata Group stability, though equity upside is more limited than at venture-backed startups. For PMs interested in health, operations-heavy consumer products and a company now growing from a profitable core, it is an attractive option.

Before accepting, confirm your level, the variable pay formula and any notice-period buyout support in writing.

## FAQs

### How much does an associate PM earn at Tata 1mg?

Estimates for 2026 put associate PM total compensation at about ₹12–26 lakh per year, depending on background, campus or lateral hiring, and negotiation. AmbitionBox reports about ₹11.4–28.5 lakh.

### Does Tata 1mg give ESOPs to product managers?

1mg had an ESOP programme before Tata Digital's acquisition. Today, long-term incentives vary by level and are more common for senior roles. Ask the recruiter what applies to your offer.

### How does Tata 1mg PM pay compare with PharmEasy or Apollo 24/7?

Tata 1mg's PM pay is broadly comparable with other health-tech companies at similar levels. Differences usually come from equity, bonuses and level mapping rather than fixed pay.

### Do diagnostics PMs earn more than pharmacy PMs at 1mg?

Pay depends mainly on level rather than charter. However, PMs on high-impact charters such as diagnostics or growth may progress faster, which affects pay over time.
