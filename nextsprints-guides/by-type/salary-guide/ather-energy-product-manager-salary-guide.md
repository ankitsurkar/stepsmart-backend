## Introduction

Ather Energy is one of India's most attractive employers for product managers who want to work on physical products as well as software. Its PMs shape vehicle software (AtherStack), the Ather app, Ather Grid charging and the ownership journey for India's third-largest electric two-wheeler maker. Since its 2025 stock market listing, Ather's compensation has combined competitive fixed pay with stock-based incentives that now have a visible market value.

This guide covers 2026 PM salary ranges in INR by level, how compensation is structured, how location and team affect pay, career progression and how to negotiate. All figures are estimates anchored on self-reported data from AmbitionBox and other public sources, so treat them as ranges, not guarantees.

## Ather Energy Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, total annual compensation for Ather PMs ranges from about ₹14 lakh for associate PMs to over ₹1 crore for heads of product. Public data points:

- AmbitionBox lists Ather Energy Product Manager pay at roughly ₹18–35 lakh per year for 3–13 years of experience, based on a small sample, and estimates it at about 18% above the average PM salary in India.
- AmbitionBox lists Product Owner pay at roughly ₹12–41 lakh (very small sample).
- AmbitionBox lists a Head of Product data point at roughly ₹66.5–73.5 lakh for 14 years of experience (single data point).
- Ather's overall AmbitionBox rating is about 4.0 out of 5, though some reviews give compensation lower marks than culture and learning.

Factors that influence pay:

- Level and years of product experience
- Domain: vehicle software, connected services and data-heavy roles often command more
- Technical background: engineering and hardware-software experience is valued
- Competing offers, especially from consumer internet companies
- Stock grant size, which varies more than fixed pay

Compared with the market, Ather pays above most traditional automotive companies and roughly in line with mid-sized Indian consumer tech companies on fixed pay. It usually pays less than top consumer internet companies and global tech firms in cash, but stock grants in a growing listed company can narrow the gap.

## Understanding Ather Energy's PM Compensation Structure

1. Fixed Salary: The largest component, typically 80–90% of total pay for junior and mid-level PMs Includes basic pay, allowances, provident fund and gratuity Reviewed annually
2. Performance Bonus: Typically 8–15% of fixed pay, linked to individual and company performance Senior roles may have a larger variable share
3. Stock-Based Compensation: Ather has granted ESOPs to employees for years; after the 2025 listing, these have a public market value Grants usually vest over about four years, often with a one-year cliff Value depends on share price, which can move significantly (Ather shares rose sharply after Q1 FY27 results in August 2026)
4. Joining Bonus: Sometimes offered to offset unvested stock or bonuses forfeited at a previous employer
5. Benefits: Health insurance for family, term life and accident cover Employee purchase programmes for Ather scooters and accessories (reported; terms vary) Learning budgets and leave policies

### Associate/Junior PM

- 2–4 years of experience, often from engineering, analytics or early product roles
- Works on a defined feature area, such as app onboarding or a service booking flow
- Strong SQL and specification-writing skills expected

### Product Manager

- 4–7 years of experience; owns a feature area end to end, for example ride experience or charging in the app
- AmbitionBox's ₹18–35 lakh band covers mostly this level and early Senior PM

### Senior PM

- 7–10 years; owns a product line such as AtherStack features, Ather Grid or the ownership journey
- Leads multiple squads and works directly with hardware and business leaders

### Principal PM

- 10+ years; sets direction for a platform (for example, connected-vehicle data or the EL platform's software)
- Influences company-level roadmap decisions

### Director of Product

- 12+ years; leads a portfolio and builds the PM team
- Total compensation can exceed ₹1 crore with stock

- These ranges are approximate and vary by team, performance and negotiation.
- Stock grants usually vest over four years; their value depends on Ather's share price.
- Exceptional candidates or critical roles may be paid above these ranges.

## How Location Affects Ather Energy PM Salaries

Almost all of Ather's product roles are based in Bengaluru, where the headquarters and R&D teams sit. Pay is set against the Bengaluru market, one of India's most competitive for PMs. Roles linked to manufacturing (Hosur in Tamil Nadu and the new plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra) are usually operations or engineering rather than product roles; any PM roles near plants generally follow Bengaluru bands with travel. Remote work is limited because PMs work closely with hardware and test teams.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Typical progression:

| Step | Time in role | Pay change at promotion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Associate PM → PM | 2–3 years | 20–30% |
| PM → Senior PM | 2–3 years | 20–30% |
| Senior PM → Principal PM | 3–4 years | 15–25% |
| Principal PM → Director | 3+ years | 20–30% |

- Annual increments outside promotions typically range from 8% to 12%.
- PMs who own launches of major features or platforms (for example, a new scooter's software) tend to progress faster.

Growth factors: impact on visible launches, cross-functional leadership with hardware teams, and the company's growth. As Ather expands with the EL platform, new product lines may create leadership roles.

## Negotiating Your Ather Energy PM Offer

1. Research: use AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and conversations with current PMs to anchor your expectations.
2. Understand stock: ask for the number of shares or options, the grant price, vesting schedule and refresh policy. Compute value at current share price, but also at lower prices.
3. Negotiate fixed pay first: it is the most predictable part and the base for future increments.
4. Use competing offers carefully: offers from consumer internet companies can help, especially for software PM roles.
5. Ask for a joining bonus to cover forfeited bonuses or unvested stock.
6. Clarify level: a higher level often matters more than a small increase in fixed pay.

> Negotiation Tip
> Explain what you bring that is hard to find, such as experience with connected devices, OTA software, hardware-software products or marketplaces, and link it to Ather's current priorities, such as the EL platform or Ather Grid.

## Conclusion

Ather Energy offers PMs competitive Bengaluru pay, stock in a fast-growing listed company and rare experience building physical products with software at their core. Cash pay may trail top internet companies, but the learning and potential stock upside make Ather attractive for PMs interested in mobility and hardware.

## FAQs

### How much does an associate PM earn at Ather Energy?

Estimates for 2026 put associate PM total compensation at about ₹14–24 lakh per year, depending on experience, background and negotiation, with some stock for stronger candidates.

### Does Ather Energy give stock to product managers?

Ather has a long-standing ESOP programme, and since its 2025 listing, grants have a public market value. Most mid-level and senior PM offers include stock that vests over about four years; ask for exact details in your offer.

### How does Ather PM pay compare with Ola Electric or TVS?

Ather's PM cash pay is generally above traditional two-wheeler makers like TVS and comparable to EV startups. Ola Electric and Ather compete for similar talent in Bengaluru, and offers can be close; the main differences are often stock value and team culture.

### Do software PMs earn more than vehicle PMs at Ather?

Pay depends mainly on level, but candidates with strong software and data skills, especially in connected services or AI, may command higher offers because they also have options at internet companies.
