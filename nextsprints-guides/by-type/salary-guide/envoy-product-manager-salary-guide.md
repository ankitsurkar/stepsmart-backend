## Understanding Envoy's PM Compensation Structure

Envoy's PM compensation structure in 2026 consists of four main components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, typically ranging from $110,000 to $250,000 depending on the PM's level and experience.
2. **Annual Bonus**: Envoy offers performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10% to 25% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company performance metrics.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Stock options or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are a significant part of Envoy's PM packages, especially for more senior roles. The equity component can range from $50,000 to $200,000+ in value annually, vesting over a four-year period.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: Envoy provides a comprehensive benefits package including: Health, dental, and vision insurance 401(k) matching Flexible work arrangements Professional development stipends Generous paid time off Parental leave Wellness programs

The base salary provides stable income, while the bonus and equity components align PM interests with company success. Envoy's benefits package is designed to support work-life balance and long-term career growth.

## Envoy Product Manager Salaries by Level

Here's a detailed breakdown of Envoy's PM salary ranges by level in 2026:

| Level | Base Salary Range | Target Bonus | Annual Equity Grant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Associate PM | $110,000 - $130,000 | 10% | $30,000 - $50,000 |
| Product Manager | $180,000 - $225,000 | 15% | $50,000 - $100,000 |
| Senior PM | $200,000 - $240,000 | 20% | $100,000 - $150,000 |
| Principal PM | $200,000 - $250,000 | 25% | $150,000 - $250,000 |
| Director of Product | $220,000 - $280,000 | 30% | $200,000 - $400,000 |

**Associate/Junior PM**: Entry-level positions focusing on learning the ropes of product management. They often work on smaller features or assist senior PMs on larger projects.

**Product Manager**: Mid-level PMs who own significant product features or smaller products. They're expected to drive product strategy and execution independently.

**Senior PM**: Experienced managers who lead major product initiatives, often managing a team of junior PMs or working cross-functionally with multiple teams.

**Principal PM**: Top individual contributors who drive critical, company-wide product strategies. They often mentor other PMs and influence overall product direction.

**Director of Product**: Leaders who oversee multiple product lines or a significant portion of Envoy's product portfolio. They're responsible for long-term product vision and team development.

It's important to note that these ranges can overlap, and individual compensation may vary based on performance, experience, and negotiation.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Envoy follows a typical path:

## FAQs

### How often does Envoy review and adjust PM salaries?

Envoy typically conducts annual performance reviews and salary adjustments. However, exceptional performance or market changes may lead to off-cycle reviews. PMs can expect a thorough evaluation of their contributions and market positioning during these reviews.

### Does Envoy offer relocation packages for PMs?

Yes, Envoy provides relocation assistance for PMs moving to work at one of their office locations. The package typically includes moving expenses, temporary housing, and sometimes a one-time relocation bonus. The specifics depend on the role level and distance of the move.

### How does Envoy's PM compensation compare to other tech companies in 2026?

Envoy's compensation is competitive with other mid-sized tech companies. Levels.fyi shows a median total compensation of roughly $200,000 for Envoy PMs in San Francisco, which is competitive with mid-sized tech peers though below the largest tech companies.

### Can Envoy PMs work remotely full-time in 2026?

As of 2026, Envoy has embraced a hybrid work model. While many PM roles offer significant flexibility for remote work, some positions may require periodic in-office presence for collaboration and team building. The specific arrangements depend on the role and team needs.
