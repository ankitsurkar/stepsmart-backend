## Understanding Character.ai's PM Compensation Structure

Character.ai's PM compensation package is designed to be both competitive and aligned with long-term company success. The structure consists of four main components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, providing a stable income that reflects the PM's experience, skills, and responsibilities. Base salaries at Character.ai are reviewed annually and adjusted based on performance and market conditions.
2. Annual Bonus: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, which vary by level. Bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company-wide performance metrics, encouraging alignment with Character.ai's strategic goals.
3. Equity Compensation: A significant portion of PM compensation comes in the form of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) or stock options. This aligns PMs' interests with the company's long-term success and can represent substantial value as Character.ai continues to grow. Equity grants are typically vested over a four-year period with a one-year cliff.
4. Benefits and Perks: Character.ai offers a comprehensive benefits package including: Health, dental, and vision insurance 401(k) matching Generous paid time off Professional development budget AI-powered mental health support Remote work flexibility Annual AI conference attendance

This multi-faceted approach ensures that PMs are well-compensated in the short term while also having a stake in the company's future success.

Character.ai's global compensation philosophy aims to balance fairness, competitiveness, and the realities of local market conditions.
