## How Location Affects FloSports PM Salaries

FloSports, like many tech companies, adjusts its compensation based on the location of its employees. This approach allows the company to remain competitive in various markets while managing costs effectively. Here's how location impacts PM salaries at FloSports:

1. Major Tech Hubs (e.g., San Francisco, New York):

- Salaries are at the higher end of the ranges, often 10-20% above the median
- Cost of living adjustments are built into base salaries
- Example: A Senior PM in San Francisco might earn $180,000 - $220,000 base salary

1. Secondary Tech Markets (e.g., Austin, Seattle):

- Salaries are typically at or slightly above the median ranges
- Reflects lower cost of living compared to major hubs but still competitive tech scenes
- Example: A Senior PM in Austin might earn $160,000 - $200,000 base salary

1. Emerging Tech Cities (e.g., Denver, Atlanta):

- Salaries are around the median or slightly below
- Balanced against lower living costs and growing tech communities
- Example: A Senior PM in Denver might earn $150,000 - $190,000 base salary

1. Remote Work:

- FloSports has offered some location-flexible roles, though policies vary by team and role, so confirm with the recruiter
- Remote salaries are typically based on the employee's actual location, not FloSports' office locations
- Remote pay is generally not higher than in-office pay for the same market

1. International PM Compensation:

- For international roles, FloSports benchmarks against local market rates
- Equity components may be adjusted based on local regulations and tax implications
- Example: A Senior PM in London might have a salary range of £120,000 - £160,000, with equity adjusted for UK regulations

> Remote Work Trend
> FloSports has offered some remote and location-flexible roles, which widens its talent pool. Check each job posting for the current remote or hybrid arrangement, as this varies by team.

It's important to note that while location affects base salary, other components like equity grants and bonuses are generally consistent across locations for the same role level.

## Negotiating Your FloSports PM Offer

Negotiating a job offer at FloSports requires a strategic approach. Here are some tips to help you navigate the process effectively:

1. Understand the full package:

- Look beyond just the base salary
- Consider the value of equity, bonuses, and benefits

1. Research thoroughly:

- Know the market rates for your level and location
- Understand FloSports' position in the market and recent growth

1. Highlight your unique value:

- Emphasize any specialized skills in sports tech or streaming
- Quantify your past achievements and their impact

1. Be clear about your expectations:

- Provide a salary range rather than a specific number
- Express enthusiasm for the role while being firm on your worth

1. Consider multiple components:

- If base salary is constrained, negotiate for higher equity or signing bonus
- Explore flexible work arrangements or additional PTO

1. Use competing offers wisely:

- If you have other offers, mention them professionally
- Focus on the opportunity at FloSports rather than just competing numbers

> Negotiation Don'ts
> Don't bluff about other offers
> Avoid ultimatums or aggressive tactics
> Don't neglect other important aspects of the role beyond compensation

Typically negotiable aspects at FloSports:

- Base salary (usually within 10-15% of the initial offer)
- Equity grants
- Signing bonuses
- Start date and relocation assistance

Less flexible components often include:

- Standard benefits packages
- Annual bonus percentages (these are usually standardized by level)

Remember, the goal is to reach a mutually beneficial agreement. FloSports values its employees and aims to provide competitive compensation, but also needs to maintain internal equity.
