## Dream11 Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, Dream11 is operating as a smaller, restructured sports-entertainment business after India's 2025 online real-money gaming ban, and its parent Dream Sports has said it will not run mass layoffs while some employees have still left. Hiring is therefore more selective than in the fantasy-sports boom years. The general salary range for Product Managers at Dream11 spans from ₹15 lakhs to ₹80 lakhs per annum, depending on various factors such as experience, level, performance, and location.

These figures are 2026 estimates based on AmbitionBox and Levels.fyi salary data, where AmbitionBox shows Dream11 Product Manager pay of roughly ₹26-45 lakhs per annum and Levels.fyi shows median total compensation of roughly ₹37 lakhs; ranges for other levels are extrapolated and less certain.

Several key factors influence PM salaries at Dream11:

1. Experience: Years of relevant product management experience, both within and outside Dream11.
2. Performance: Individual and team contributions to product success and company growth.
3. Location: Compensation adjustments based on the cost of living in different cities.
4. Education: Advanced degrees or specialized certifications may impact salary offers.
5. Skills: Expertise in specific domains or technologies relevant to Dream11's products.

Compared to industry standards, Dream11's PM compensation has generally been on par with or somewhat above other major Indian consumer-tech companies. Since the 2025 real-money gaming ban, however, the company's revenue base has shrunk sharply, so candidates should treat equity and bonus upside with more caution than in earlier years.

## Understanding Dream11's PM Compensation Structure

Dream11's PM compensation package is designed to be competitive and rewarding, consisting of several key components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the core of the compensation package, typically ranging from ₹15 lakhs to ₹50 lakhs per annum, depending on the PM's level and experience.
2. Annual Bonus: Dream11 offers performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10% to 30% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company performance metrics.
3. Equity Compensation: Stock options or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are a significant part of Dream11's PM compensation, especially at higher levels. The equity share of total compensation grows with seniority, and grants commonly vest over about four years; confirm the exact vesting terms and the current valuation basis in your offer, especially given the post-ban restructuring.
4. Benefits and Perks: Health insurance for employees and dependents Life and disability insurance Retirement benefits (Provident Fund and Gratuity) Flexible work arrangements Professional development budget Gym memberships or wellness allowances Annual sports events and team-building activities
5. Sign-on Bonus: For highly sought-after candidates, Dream11 may offer a one-time sign-on bonus, though this is negotiated case by case.

The combination of these components allows Dream11 to offer attractive total compensation packages while aligning PM incentives with the company's long-term success.

## How Location Affects Dream11 PM Salaries

While Dream11's headquarters is in Mumbai, the company has historically followed a mostly in-office model with some flexibility, which impacts how location factors into PM salaries:

1. Major Tech Hubs (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR): PMs in these locations typically receive the full salary range for their level. Cost of living adjustments may result in slightly higher salaries in Mumbai compared to other cities.
2. Tier 2 Cities (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai): Salaries may be 5-10% lower than those in major tech hubs. This difference is often offset by a lower cost of living.
3. Remote Work: Remote arrangements are limited and handled case by case; where offered, pay is typically aligned with tier 2 city compensation. Remote employees may receive additional allowances for home office setup and internet costs.
4. International PMs: For PMs based outside India, Dream11 adjusts salaries based on local market rates and cost of living. Expatriate packages may include additional benefits like relocation assistance and housing allowances.

Dream11's location-based compensation strategy aims to balance fairness across different regions while remaining competitive in each local market.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Dream11 is based on a combination of performance, impact, and tenure. Here's a typical career path for PMs:

1. Associate PM → PM: 2-3 years
2. PM → Senior PM: 3-4 years
3. Senior PM → Principal PM: 3-5 years
4. Principal PM → Director of Product: 3-5 years

Salary growth at Dream11 is tied to both individual performance and company success. On average, PMs can expect:

- Annual merit increases: typically single-digit to low double-digit percentages, depending on performance
- Promotion increases: usually a meaningful bump in base salary, with larger equity grants
- Company performance bonuses: Can boost total compensation when company results are strong

Dream11 also offers fast-track programs for exceptional performers, allowing for more rapid advancement and corresponding salary increases.

## Negotiating Your Dream11 PM Offer

When negotiating a PM offer at Dream11, consider these tips:

1. Research thoroughly: Understand Dream11's compensation structure and industry benchmarks.
2. Highlight your unique value: Emphasize skills or experiences that directly align with Dream11's needs.
3. Consider the total package: Look beyond base salary to bonus potential, equity, and benefits.
4. Be realistic but ambitious: Aim for the upper end of the salary range for your level.

Negotiable aspects often include:

- Equity grants (especially for senior roles)
- Sign-on bonuses
- Performance bonus targets
- Professional development budgets

- Don't make ultimatums or bluff about other offers
- Be prepared to justify your requests with data and achievements

## FAQs

### How often does Dream11 review and adjust PM salaries?

Dream11 conducts periodic performance reviews and salary adjustments. Exact timing and any off-cycle reviews vary, so confirm the current cycle with your recruiter.

### Does Dream11 offer different compensation for PMs specializing in specific sports or technologies?

Yes, Dream11 may offer premium compensation for PMs with expertise in emerging technologies like AI/ML or for those with deep knowledge in specific sports that are strategic to the company's growth plans. The size of any premium is negotiated case by case.

### How does Dream11's PM compensation compare to other major Indian startups?

Dream11's PM compensation is generally competitive with other well-funded Indian startups. While base salaries might be slightly lower than some global tech giants, equity value is now harder to predict after the 2025 real-money gaming ban, so weigh it cautiously against cash.

### What happens to equity compensation if Dream11 goes public?

Dream11 has not announced an IPO, and the 2025 real-money gaming ban makes a near-term listing unlikely. If a listing did happen, options or RSUs would typically convert into public shares subject to lock-up periods; check your grant documents for the governing terms.
