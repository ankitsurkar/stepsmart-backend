## Introduction

Cohere is one of the best-funded independent AI companies outside the largest US labs, and product managers there work on some of the most technical products in enterprise software: language models, retrieval models, private deployment platforms and the North agentic workspace. Because Cohere hires in both Canada and the United States, its pay is quoted in two currencies, and the gap between them can be large. This guide gives 2026 salary estimates by level in USD and CAD, explains how Cohere structures compensation, and offers negotiation tips.

## Cohere Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, Cohere PM base salaries generally range from about USD 150,000 for a Product Manager to more than USD 300,000 for directors in US offices. In Toronto, base salaries generally range from about CAD 140,000 to over CAD 300,000. Total compensation adds stock options, which can be significant but are illiquid until a sale or public listing.

These estimates are anchored on:

- Public job postings: recent PM roles listed base ranges such as USD 140,000-210,000 (Product Manager, Search and Embeddings) and USD 160,000-285,000 (Product Manager, Managed North, Canada or US).
- Levels.fyi data for Cohere in Canada, showing a median Product Manager total compensation of around CAD 156,000, with the highest reported packages above CAD 220,000.

Factors influencing salary:

- Level and scope (individual product versus product area)
- Location (San Francisco and New York pay more than Toronto in absolute terms)
- Technical depth (model, retrieval or infrastructure experience is in demand)
- Competing offers from other AI companies

Compared with the market, Cohere pays at or above typical enterprise software companies, and generally below the very top frontier labs once equity is included.

## Understanding Cohere's PM Compensation Structure

1. Base salary: The largest and most predictable component. Set within a posted range by level and location; North American postings show ranges by law in many jurisdictions.
2. Equity: Stock options in a private company, typically vesting over four years with a one-year cliff (standard for venture-backed companies; exact terms vary). Value depends on future funding rounds, secondary sales or an IPO. Recent private valuations were reported around USD 6.8 billion in 2025, with later reports of a higher valuation in 2026.
3. Bonus: Not a major component for most PM roles; some senior roles may have performance-linked elements.
4. Benefits: Health, dental and vision coverage; in Canada, supplementary coverage on top of provincial health care. Learning budgets, parental leave and hybrid working in office hubs. Retirement savings matching where offered (401(k) in the US, RRSP in Canada; varies).

> Equity Insight
> Private-company options are worth asking about in detail: strike price, number of shares, latest preferred price, and exercise window after leaving. Two offers with similar "equity values" can be very different in practice.

### Product Manager

- Typically 3-5 years of product experience, often from developer tools, search or enterprise SaaS.
- Owns a feature area such as a connector set, an evaluation tool or an onboarding flow.
- Toronto offers commonly fall around CAD 150,000-170,000 base.

### Senior Product Manager

- 5-8 years of experience; owns a product such as Rerank, Compass or a North agent area.
- Works directly with design-partner customers and research leads.

### Staff / Principal PM

- Owns a major product line or cross-cutting platform (for example private deployment).
- Shapes strategy with leadership and represents Cohere with strategic customers.

### Group PM / Director

- Manages a team of PMs across a product area.
- Accountable for revenue-related outcomes such as adoption and expansion.

## Negotiating Your Cohere PM Offer

1. Know the posted range. Cohere postings often include a base range; anchor near the top if your experience matches the senior end.
2. Clarify the level. The biggest pay difference comes from being levelled as Senior instead of PM.
3. Ask about equity details: number of options, strike price and latest valuation.
4. Use competing offers carefully. Offers from other AI companies carry weight; be factual.
5. Negotiate non-salary items: start date, signing bonus, remote flexibility and learning budget.
6. For cross-border moves, ask about relocation and immigration support.

> Negotiation Tip
> If base salary is fixed by the band, ask for a higher equity grant or a signing bonus. Startups often have more flexibility there.

## FAQs

### Does Cohere pay Canadian PMs less than US PMs?

In absolute terms, yes. Cohere sets pay by location, and Toronto ranges in CAD are lower than US ranges in USD. Relative to each local market, though, Cohere's Toronto offers are among the strongest for product managers in Canada.

### How much is Cohere equity worth?

It depends on the company's future. Cohere's options are private-company equity; their value is realised only through a sale, secondary transaction or public listing. Treat equity as upside and evaluate offers primarily on base salary.

### How does Cohere compare with OpenAI or Anthropic on pay?

The largest US labs generally offer higher total compensation, mostly through equity. Cohere's base salaries are competitive with large tech companies, but total packages are usually lower than at the very top labs.

### Is there an entry-level PM salary at Cohere?

Cohere rarely hires PMs straight from university. Early-career candidates usually join in engineering, solutions or operations roles and move into product later, so salaries for those roles are the more relevant benchmark.
