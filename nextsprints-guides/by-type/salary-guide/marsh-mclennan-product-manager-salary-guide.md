## Understanding Marsh McLennan's PM Compensation Structure

Marsh McLennan's compensation structure for Product Managers is designed to be competitive and reward performance. It consists of several key components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the foundation of the compensation package and is determined based on the PM's level, experience, and location.
2. Annual Bonus: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, typically ranging from 10-30% of base salary, depending on individual and company performance.
3. Equity Compensation: Marsh McLennan offers restricted stock units (RSUs) to PMs, with grant sizes increasing at higher levels. Vesting usually occurs over a 3-4 year period.
4. Benefits and Perks: These include comprehensive health insurance, retirement plans, professional development budgets, and flexible work arrangements.

Here's how each component works:

- Base Salary: Reviewed annually, with potential increases based on performance and market conditions.
- Annual Bonus: Tied to both individual and company-wide key performance indicators (KPIs), paid out yearly.
- Equity: RSUs are granted annually, with the number of units based on the PM's level and performance. The value fluctuates with Marsh McLennan's stock price.
- Benefits: Includes standard offerings plus unique perks like sabbatical opportunities after certain tenure milestones.

> Insider Tip
> Equity is a smaller part of PM pay at Marsh McLennan than at pure tech companies, and cash base and bonus usually make up most of total compensation, so weigh the whole package when comparing offers.
