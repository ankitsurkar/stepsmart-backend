## Understanding Realtor.com's PM Compensation Structure

Realtor.com's PM compensation package is designed to provide a mix of immediate and long-term incentives. Here's a breakdown of the key components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, paid bi-weekly or monthly. Base salaries are determined by level, experience, and location.
2. **Annual Bonus**: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, typically ranging from 10-25% of base salary, depending on individual and company performance.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Realtor.com offers Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) that vest over a 4-year period, aligning PM interests with long-term company success.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: These include health insurance, 401(k) matching, professional development budgets, and work-from-home allowances.

Here's how each component works:

- Base salary is reviewed annually, with potential increases based on performance and market conditions.
- Annual bonuses are calculated using a combination of individual OKRs and company-wide metrics.
- RSUs are granted at hire and refreshed annually, with the amount varying by level and performance.
- Benefits are standardized across levels but may have options for customization.

> Equity Vesting Schedule
> Typical RSU grants vest over 4 years, with 25% vesting at the one-year cliff and the remainder vesting quarterly thereafter.

## Negotiating Your Realtor.com PM Offer

When negotiating a PM offer at realtor.com, consider these tips:

1. **Research thoroughly**: Understand the full compensation package and how it compares to market rates.
2. **Prioritize your asks**: Decide what's most important to you (base salary, equity, sign-on bonus, etc.).
3. **Highlight your unique value**: Emphasize specific skills or experiences that align with realtor.com's needs.
4. **Be professional and collaborative**: Approach negotiations as a partnership, not an adversarial process.

Aspects typically open to negotiation:

- Base salary (within the role's predetermined range)
- Equity grants
- Sign-on bonuses
- Flexible work arrangements

Common mistakes to avoid:

- Focusing solely on base salary without considering the total package
- Making ultimatums or using aggressive tactics
- Neglecting to discuss performance expectations and growth opportunities

> Negotiation Etiquette
> Always conduct negotiations professionally and be prepared to provide rationale for your requests. Realtor.com values transparency and fairness in its compensation practices.
