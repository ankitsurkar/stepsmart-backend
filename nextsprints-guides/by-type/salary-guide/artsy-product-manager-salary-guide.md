These figures are 2026 estimates based on Artsy's posted job listings and Comparably salary data.

This multi-faceted approach ensures that PMs are well-compensated while also enjoying unique benefits that reflect Artsy's position in the art world. Equity is part of the package, though its value is uncertain for a private company.

## How Location Affects Artsy PM Salaries

Artsy is headquartered in New York, and recent PM postings there are on-site. Location can still affect pay:

1. Major Tech Hubs: PMs based in high-cost areas like New York, San Francisco, or London may see higher pay than other markets to account for increased living costs.
2. Remote Work: Remote arrangements are less common and may carry location-based pay adjustments.
3. International Compensation: For international PMs, Artsy benchmarks salaries against local market rates while ensuring they remain competitive within the global tech industry. This may result in significant variations depending on the country.
4. Relocation Support: Artsy offers relocation packages for PMs moving to key office locations, which can include moving expenses and temporary housing support.

While location does play a role in determining salary, Artsy strives to maintain equity across its global workforce by focusing on the value each PM brings to the company, regardless of their physical location.

Artsy conducts annual salary reviews, typically coinciding with performance evaluations. However, the company also monitors market trends and may make off-cycle adjustments to ensure competitiveness. PMs can expect a thorough review of their compensation package at least once a year, with potential for more frequent adjustments based on exceptional performance or significant market shifts.

### Does Artsy offer any unique compensation perks related to the art world?

Artsy's culture is closely tied to the art world, and employees may have access to art-related perks and events. Specific perks are not published and can change, so ask your recruiter what is currently offered.

Artsy's product organization is relatively small, which gives PMs broad scope and exposure across product areas. Formal programs vary, so ask about mentorship, learning budgets and growth paths during the interview process.
