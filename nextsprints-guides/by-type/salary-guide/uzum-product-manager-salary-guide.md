## Understanding Uzum's PM Compensation Structure

Uzum's compensation structure for Product Managers is designed to be competitive, motivating, and aligned with the company's long-term growth objectives. The package typically consists of the following components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, paid monthly and determined based on the PM's level, experience, and performance.
2. Annual Bonus: Uzum offers performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10% to 30% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company-wide performance metrics.
3. Equity Compensation: As a privately held company, Uzum may offer stock options or restricted stock units (RSUs) to PMs, especially at higher levels. This aligns employees' interests with the company's long-term success.
4. Benefits and Perks: Uzum provides a comprehensive benefits package, including: Health insurance Paid time off Professional development allowances Flexible work arrangements Employee discounts on Uzum's platform
5. Sign-on Bonus: For highly sought-after candidates or those relocating, Uzum may offer a one-time sign-on bonus.

The base salary provides stability, while the annual bonus and equity components incentivize performance and long-term commitment. As Uzum continues to grow, the equity component could become increasingly valuable, potentially offering significant upside for early employees.

## How Location Affects Uzum PM Salaries

While Uzum is primarily based in Uzbekistan, the company's expansion efforts may lead to location-based salary adjustments. As of 2026, here's how location impacts PM compensation at Uzum:

1. Tashkent (Headquarters): Base salaries are calibrated to the local market, forming the benchmark for other locations. PMs in Tashkent receive the full benefits package and equity offerings.
2. Regional Uzbek Cities: Salaries may be lower than Tashkent, reflecting lower costs of living. Full benefits package is maintained.
3. International Offices (e.g., Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan): Salaries are adjusted based on local market rates and cost of living. Pay follows local market rates, which can differ from Tashkent.
4. Remote Work: Uzum offers remote work options with location-based pay adjustments. Remote pay is typically set with reference to the Tashkent benchmark and the employee's location.

As Uzum expands its international presence, the company aims to remain competitive in each market while maintaining internal equity across its global workforce.
