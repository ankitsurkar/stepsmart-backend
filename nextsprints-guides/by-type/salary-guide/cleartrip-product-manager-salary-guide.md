## Introduction

Cleartrip, the Flipkart-owned online travel company, hires product managers across flights, hotels, trains and buses, payments and offers, Cleartrip for Business, and its integration with the Flipkart app. Because it sits inside the Flipkart group, its pay is often compared with Flipkart's, but it is a separate business with its own budget pressures. The company is targeting operational breakeven by the end of FY27, which shapes how it hires and pays. This guide gives 2026 salary estimates for Cleartrip PMs by level in Indian rupees, explains how pay is structured, and offers negotiation tips.

## Cleartrip Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, total compensation for Cleartrip product managers ranges from roughly ₹18 lakh per year for associate PMs to over ₹1.5 crore for senior product leaders.

Self-reported data on AmbitionBox puts Cleartrip "Product Manager" salaries at roughly ₹22–40 lakh per year, and "Product Manager 2" at about ₹24–32 lakh, though sample sizes are small and some data is from 2023–2024. Levels.fyi data for Flipkart, Cleartrip's parent, shows total compensation of around ₹21 lakh at Associate PM I, about ₹44 lakh at PM I and about ₹60 lakh at PM II in India. Cleartrip offers generally sit at or slightly below Flipkart's for equivalent levels.

Factors influencing salary:

- Level and years of experience
- Prior employer (consumer internet, e-commerce and other OTAs command more)
- Area of ownership (core flights and payments roles may be valued higher)
- Campus pedigree for early-career hires
- Whether long-term incentives are part of the offer

Compared with the market, Cleartrip pays in line with mid-to-large Indian consumer internet companies. It is usually above smaller travel startups and traditional travel companies, roughly comparable to listed peers like ixigo, and somewhat below Flipkart core roles and top global tech firms.

## Understanding Cleartrip's PM Compensation Structure

1. Fixed pay: The main component, typically 80–90% of cash compensation at junior and mid levels Includes basic pay, allowances and provident fund contributions Reviewed annually
2. Performance bonus: Typically 10–15% of fixed pay at junior levels and up to 20% at senior levels Linked to individual rating and company performance Usually paid annually
3. Long-term incentives: Stock-linked incentives are reported in Flipkart group offers, especially at PM and above; structure for Cleartrip roles varies and should be confirmed in writing Vesting typically spreads over four years
4. Benefits: Health insurance for employee and family Life and accident cover Travel-related employee perks may apply Learning budgets and leave policies

Campus offers can include a joining bonus. Lateral offers may include a sign-on bonus to offset forfeited bonuses or stock.

### Associate Product Manager

- Entry level, typically MBA graduates or engineers moving into product
- Owns smaller features and experiments under a senior PM, such as a checkout improvement or an offers widget
- 0–2 years of experience

### Product Manager 1

- Owns a feature area like train booking, hotel search filters or post-booking servicing
- Works directly with engineering, design and data
- 2–5 years of experience

### Product Manager 2 / Senior PM

- Owns a full journey or product line, such as hotel discovery or ancillaries
- Accountable for conversion and revenue targets
- 5–8 years of experience

### Lead / Group Product Manager

- Leads several PMs across a category (for example Air or Hotels) or a platform (payments, offers)
- Sets roadmaps and works closely with business heads
- 8–12 years of experience

### Director / Head of Product

- Owns a large portfolio and product strategy
- Partners with leadership on P&L outcomes
- 12+ years of experience

- These ranges are estimates based on AmbitionBox and Levels.fyi data, adjusted for 2026.
- Long-term incentive (LTI) values depend on grant size and vesting and are not included in the totals.
- Strong candidates from top consumer internet companies may land above these bands.

## How Location Affects Cleartrip PM Salaries

- Bengaluru: Cleartrip's main product and technology hub, alongside Flipkart. The bands above are anchored here.
- Mumbai: Cleartrip's original home city. Any roles here are generally paid on similar bands.
- Middle East: Roles linked to the Gulf business are paid on local market rates and are not comparable with Indian bands.

Most product roles work from the office for part or all of the week. Location affects relocation support more than salary.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Typical progression:

1. APM → PM 1 (1.5–2 years)
2. PM 1 → PM 2 / Senior PM (2–3 years)
3. Senior PM → Lead / Group PM (3–4 years)
4. Group PM → Director (3–5 years)

Salary growth:

- Annual increments typically range from 8–15%, depending on rating and company performance
- Promotions usually bring 15–25% increases
- PMs who own visible, revenue-linked areas (hotels, ancillaries, offers) can move faster

- Moves within the Flipkart group can broaden scope, though they depend on openings and internal policies.
- Given Cleartrip's breakeven target, bonuses may be tied more closely to profitability metrics than in earlier years.

## Negotiating Your Cleartrip PM Offer

1. Benchmark against peers: Compare with Flipkart, MakeMyTrip, ixigo and other consumer internet companies at your level.
2. Clarify the level: Title and level determine bands, so confirm whether you are joining as PM 1 or PM 2.
3. Focus on fixed pay: It compounds through annual increments and is more certain than bonus.
4. Understand long-term incentives: Ask what instrument is offered, how many units, the vesting schedule and refresh practice.
5. Ask for a sign-on bonus: Use it to offset bonuses or stock you leave behind.

Negotiable items:

- Fixed pay within the level band
- Joining or sign-on bonus
- Level (for strong lateral candidates)
- Joining date and relocation support

Mistakes to avoid:

- Comparing only cash with equity-heavy startup offers
- Ignoring the bonus structure and how ratings are set
- Assuming Flipkart and Cleartrip bands are identical

## Conclusion

Cleartrip offers PMs competitive consumer internet pay, with a strong fixed component, an annual bonus and, at many levels, long-term incentives linked to the Flipkart group. Pay is broadly in line with peers like ixigo and somewhat below Flipkart's core teams. For candidates, the bigger draw may be the work itself: building hotels, trains and ancillaries at a company that must become profitable, with Flipkart's scale behind it.

## FAQs

### What is the starting salary for an APM at Cleartrip?

For associate PMs, including MBA graduates, total compensation in 2026 is estimated at around ₹18–28 lakh per year, depending on background, school and negotiation. Engineers moving into product may land at a similar level if they bring relevant experience.

### Does Cleartrip pay the same as Flipkart?

Not exactly. Cleartrip uses similar level names and benefits, but offers are usually at or slightly below Flipkart core bands for the same level, and long-term incentive structures can differ. Always compare the full offer, not just the title.

### How does Cleartrip compare with MakeMyTrip and ixigo?

Pay is broadly comparable at junior and mid levels. MakeMyTrip, as the market leader, may pay more at senior levels. ixigo, a listed company, offers stock-linked incentives. The differences are often smaller than differences in role scope and team.

### Are Cleartrip salaries affected by its losses?

Cleartrip's focus on breakeven means hiring is selective and budgets are watched closely. Increments and bonuses may track company performance more tightly, but individual offers for key product roles remain competitive.
