## Understanding Coalition's PM Compensation Structure

Coalition's PM compensation structure is designed to align employee incentives with company success and promote long-term retention. The package consists of four main components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, providing a stable income that typically increases with experience and performance.
2. **Annual Bonus**: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10-30% of base salary depending on level and individual/company performance.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Coalition is privately held and offers equity that typically vests over four years. The amount of equity granted increases with seniority and can significantly boost total compensation.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: Coalition provides a comprehensive benefits package including: Health, dental, and vision insurance 401(k) matching Generous paid time off Professional development budget Remote work options Wellness programs

Here's how each component works:

- **Base Salary**: Paid bi-weekly or monthly, reviewed annually with potential for merit increases.
- **Annual Bonus**: Calculated based on individual and company performance metrics, paid out annually.
- **Equity**: Equity is granted upon hiring; as a private company, its value is realized only through a liquidity event or secondary sale.
- **Benefits**: Immediately available upon start date, with some benefits like 401(k) matching subject to vesting periods.

This structure allows Coalition to offer competitive cash compensation while also providing long-term incentives through equity, aligning PM interests with the company's growth and success.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Coalition follows a structured path, with opportunities for salary growth tied to promotions and performance:

1. **Typical Career Path**: Associate PM → PM → Senior PM → Principal PM → Director of Product
2. **Salary Increases**: Annual merit increases: 3-5% for meeting expectations Promotional increases: 10-20% when moving to the next level Performance-based increases: Top performers may see 7-10% annual increases

## Negotiating Your Coalition PM Offer

When negotiating a PM offer at Coalition, consider these tips:

1. **Research thoroughly**: Use this guide and external sources like [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) to understand market rates.
2. **Focus on total compensation**: Don't fixate solely on base salary; consider the full package including equity and bonuses.
3. **Highlight your unique value**: Emphasize specific skills or experiences that align with Coalition's needs.
4. **Be prepared to justify your ask**: Provide concrete examples of your impact in previous roles.
5. **Consider trade-offs**: If base salary is constrained, negotiate for more equity or a signing bonus.

Typically negotiable aspects include:

- Base salary (within the role's range)
- Equity grants
- Signing bonus
- Start date and first review timeline

Common mistakes to avoid:

- Accepting the first offer without negotiation
- Focusing only on base salary and ignoring long-term value of equity
- Making ultimatums or using aggressive tactics

> Negotiation Etiquette
> Always negotiate professionally and express enthusiasm for the role. Coalition values transparency and fairness in its compensation practices.

### How often are PM salaries reviewed at Coalition?

Coalition typically conducts annual performance and salary reviews for all employees, including Product Managers. These reviews usually take place at the end of the fiscal year, with any salary adjustments or promotions becoming effective at the start of the new fiscal year. However, exceptional performers or those taking on significantly expanded responsibilities may be considered for off-cycle reviews and adjustments.

Yes, Coalition offers relocation packages for Product Managers who are required to move for their role. The specifics of the package can vary based on the level of the PM, the distance of the move, and whether it's a domestic or international relocation. Typically, these packages may include:
