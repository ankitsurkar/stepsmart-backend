## Understanding HireRight's PM Compensation Structure

HireRight's Product Manager compensation package is designed to be comprehensive and competitive, consisting of several key components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, typically ranging from $90,000 to $200,000+ depending on the PM's level and experience.
2. **Annual Bonus**: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10% to 25% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company performance metrics.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Since General Atlantic and Stone Point Capital took HireRight private in 2024, equity is no longer publicly traded stock; any long-term incentive is typically tied to private-company equity and varies by role, so confirm the details in your offer. The equity grant value typically increases with seniority.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance 401(k) matching program Professional development budget Flexible work arrangements Paid time off and parental leave

Here's how each component works:

- Base salary is paid bi-weekly and reviewed annually.
- Annual bonuses are calculated at the end of the fiscal year and paid out in Q1.
- Equity typically vests over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff.
- Benefits are available from the start of employment, with some having short waiting periods.

> Insider Tip
> HireRight has been known to offer sign-on bonuses for senior PM roles to attract top talent, especially in competitive markets.

## How Location Affects HireRight PM Salaries

Location plays a significant role in determining PM salaries at HireRight. As of 2026, the company maintains a hybrid work model, with offices in major tech hubs and remote options available.

- **Major Tech Hubs** (e.g., San Francisco Bay Area, New York City): PMs in these locations typically receive salaries at the higher end of the ranges, with additional cost of living adjustments.
- **Secondary Markets** (e.g., Austin, Denver): Salaries are generally 10-15% lower than in major hubs but still competitive for the local market.
- **Remote Work**: HireRight offers location-based pay for remote workers, with salaries adjusted based on the cost of living in the employee's area.

> Remote Work Consideration
> While remote work offers flexibility, it may impact your overall compensation if you're based in a lower cost of living area compared to major tech hubs.

International PM compensation at HireRight varies significantly based on the country and local market conditions. The company strives to offer competitive packages in each market while maintaining internal equity.

## FAQs

### How often does HireRight review and adjust PM salaries?

HireRight typically conducts annual salary reviews, usually coinciding with performance evaluations. However, the company also performs regular market analysis and may make off-cycle adjustments to ensure competitiveness, especially for in-demand roles or in response to significant market shifts.

### Does HireRight offer relocation packages for PMs?

Yes, HireRight offers relocation packages for PM roles, particularly for senior positions or when moving talent to key office locations. These packages can include moving expenses, temporary housing, and sometimes a relocation bonus. The specifics depend on the role level and the distance of the move.

### How does HireRight's PM compensation compare to other background screening companies?

HireRight generally offers competitive compensation compared to other players in the background screening industry. While base salaries might be slightly lower than some tech giants, the total compensation package, including bonuses and equity, is often on par or better than industry peers like First Advantage (which acquired Sterling Check in 2024).

### Can PMs at HireRight expect salary increases with the acquisition of new skills or certifications?

While there's no automatic salary increase for new skills or certifications, HireRight values continuous learning and development. PMs who acquire relevant skills or certifications (e.g., CSPO, PMC) often have a stronger case for promotion or salary increases during annual reviews. The company also offers a professional development budget that can be used for acquiring these skills.
