## Understanding Pax8's PM Compensation Structure

Pax8's compensation package for Product Managers is designed to be competitive and rewarding, consisting of several key components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, typically ranging from about $85,000 to $200,000+ depending on the PM's level and experience; a recent Colorado posting for a Product Manager (Finance) listed $115,000 to $135,000.
2. **Annual Bonus**: PMs are eligible for performance-based bonuses, usually ranging from 10% to 25% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company-wide performance metrics.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Pax8 lists stock option eligibility as a benefit for full-time employees to align PM interests with long-term company success. The amount varies by level and can be a significant part of total compensation.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: These include comprehensive health insurance, 401(k) matching, professional development budgets, and flexible work arrangements.

Each component works together to create a well-rounded package:

- The base salary provides stable income
- Annual bonuses reward short-term performance and goal achievement
- Equity compensation encourages long-term commitment and aligns with company growth
- Benefits and perks support work-life balance and personal development

This structure allows Pax8 to compete for top talent while ensuring PMs are invested in the company's long-term success.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Pax8 for Product Managers typically follows this path:

1. Associate/Junior PM
2. Product Manager
3. Senior PM
4. Principal PM
5. Director of Product

Salary growth is tied closely to this progression. On average, PMs can expect:

- 10-15% increase with a promotion to the next level
- 3-5% annual merit increases, depending on performance
- Potential for larger jumps (20%+) when moving to senior leadership roles

Timeframes for advancement vary, but generally:

- Junior to PM: 1-2 years
- PM to Senior PM: 2-3 years
- Senior PM to Principal: 3-4 years
- Principal to Director: 4-5 years or more, depending on business needs and individual performance

Pax8 also values lateral moves and cross-functional experience, which can enhance a PM's skill set and potentially accelerate career growth.
