## Coda Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, Coda's Product Manager salaries are competitive within the tech industry, reflecting the company's commitment to attracting and retaining top talent. The general salary range for PMs at Coda spans from roughly S$65,000 for entry-level positions to S$200,000 or more for lead and director roles, not including bonuses and equity compensation. NodeFlair, drawing on MyCareersFuture listings and past job postings, shows an average senior PM range of about S$7,900 - S$12,300 per month in Singapore (roughly S$95,000 - S$148,000 a year). These figures are 2026 estimates informed by NodeFlair data.

Several factors influence a PM's salary at Coda:

1. Experience: Years in product management and relevant industry expertise
2. Performance: Individual and team contributions to Coda's success
3. Location: Singapore-based salaries adjusted for cost of living
4. Education: Advanced degrees or specialized certifications
5. Skills: Technical proficiency and soft skills

Compared to industry standards, Coda's PM compensation package is generally in line with other fintech and tech companies in Singapore. However, Coda often differentiates itself through its equity offerings and unique company culture, which can be particularly attractive to PMs looking for high-growth opportunities.

## Understanding Coda's PM Compensation Structure

Coda's PM compensation structure in 2026 is designed to be competitive and align with the company's values of innovation and collaboration. The package typically consists of four main components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, ranging from about S$65,000 to S$200,000+ depending on the PM's level and experience.
2. Annual Bonus: Coda offers performance-based bonuses, typically ranging from 10% to 30% of the base salary. These bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company-wide performance metrics.
3. Equity Compensation: Stock options or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are a significant part of Coda's PM compensation. Grant sizes increase with seniority and are set in each offer.
4. Benefits and Perks: Coda provides a comprehensive benefits package including: Health, dental, and vision insurance Retirement savings plans Flexible work arrangements Professional development budget Wellness programs Generous paid time off

Here's how each component works:

Base Salary: Paid bi-weekly or monthly, this is the fixed portion of compensation.

Annual Bonus: Calculated based on performance reviews and company financial results, paid out annually.

Equity Compensation: Vesting schedules are set in each offer and commonly run over four years.

Benefits and Perks: These are ongoing and may have different enrollment periods or usage guidelines.

In 2026, Coda's PM salary structure is tiered based on experience and responsibility levels. Here's a detailed breakdown:

- Base Salary: S$65,000 - S$85,000
- Annual Bonus: 10-15% of base salary
- Equity: set in each offer

### Product Manager

- Base Salary: S$85,000 - S$115,000
- Annual Bonus: 15-20% of base salary

- Annual Bonus: 25-30% of base salary
- Equity: typically larger than at Senior PM level

- Base Salary: S$200,000+
- Annual Bonus: 30%+ of base salary
- Equity: a significant share of total pay

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Coda follows a structured path, allowing PMs to grow both professionally and financially:

1. Associate PM → PM: 1-3 years
2. PM → Senior PM: 2-4 years
3. Senior PM → Principal PM: 3-5 years
4. Principal PM → Director of Product: 4-6 years

Salary growth is tied to these promotions, with significant jumps at each level:

- Each promotion typically brings a meaningful step up in base pay and bonus, with larger jumps at the senior and lead levels

Timeframes for advancement can vary based on individual performance and company growth. Coda also offers opportunities for lateral moves into specialized PM roles or related fields like Product Marketing or Product Operations, which can impact compensation trajectories.

## FAQs

### How often does Coda review and adjust PM salaries?

Coda typically conducts annual performance reviews and salary adjustments. However, in the fast-paced tech industry of 2026, the company also performs mid-year check-ins to ensure compensation remains competitive. Exceptional performance or significant market changes may lead to off-cycle adjustments.

### Does Coda offer any unique compensation perks for PMs?

Perks beyond standard benefits vary by team and change over time, so ask your recruiter which learning, wellness, or performance-linked incentives apply to the role.

### How does Coda's PM compensation compare to other tech companies in Singapore?

While Coda's base salaries are generally in line with other major tech companies in Singapore, equity can be a larger part of the package at senior levels, reflecting the potential for long-term gains as the company grows.

### Can PMs at Coda negotiate for more equity instead of a higher base salary?

Yes, Coda is often flexible in this regard. Many PMs, especially those confident in the company's growth trajectory, opt for larger equity grants in lieu of higher base salaries. However, this should be approached carefully and with consideration of your personal financial situation and risk tolerance.
