## Introduction

ixigo (Le Travenues Technology) hires product managers across trains, flights, buses, hotels, payments and AI. Since its 2024 stock market listing and the 2025 Prosus investment, it has expanded its product and AI teams while staying profitable. Its pay reflects that position: competitive for Indian consumer internet, with a strong fixed component and stock options at many levels. This guide gives 2026 salary estimates for ixigo PMs by level in Indian rupees, explains how pay is structured, and offers negotiation tips.

## ixigo Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, total compensation for ixigo product managers ranges from roughly ₹12 lakh per year for associate PMs to over ₹1 crore for senior product leaders.

Self-reported data on AmbitionBox puts ixigo "Product Manager" salaries at roughly ₹14.5–43 lakh per year for people with 1–6 years of experience, with another listing showing about ₹11–36 lakh. Sample sizes are small (data from a few dozen employees), so treat these as directional.

Factors influencing salary:

- Level and years of experience
- Area of ownership (AI, flights and payments roles are in high demand)
- Previous employer (top consumer internet and AI backgrounds command more)
- Stock option grants
- Location within the group (Gurugram head office vs AbhiBus or ConfirmTkt teams)

Compared with the market, ixigo pays in line with mid-sized Indian consumer internet companies. It is usually below the largest players like Flipkart and MakeMyTrip at senior levels, but offers broader ownership and stock options in a listed, profitable company.

## Understanding ixigo's PM Compensation Structure

1. Fixed pay: The main component, typically 85–90% of cash compensation at junior and mid levels Includes basic pay, allowances and provident fund contributions Reviewed annually
2. Performance bonus: Typically 5–15% of fixed pay, linked to individual and company performance More significant at senior levels
3. Stock options (ESOPs): ixigo uses employee stock option schemes; as a listed company, the value of options is visible in the share price Vesting typically spreads over three to four years More common and larger at Senior PM and above
4. Benefits: Health insurance for employee and family Life and accident cover Travel-related perks may apply Learning support

Lateral offers may include a sign-on bonus to offset forfeited bonuses or stock.

### Associate Product Manager

- Entry level, including MBA graduates and engineers or analysts moving into product
- Owns features and experiments within a vertical, such as PNR status improvements or bus seat selection
- 0–2 years of experience

### Product Manager

- Owns a feature area like waitlist predictions, flight checkout or hotel cross-sell
- Works directly with engineering, data science and design
- 2–5 years of experience

### Senior Product Manager

- Owns a full product line, such as ConfirmTkt booking or ixigo Assured
- Accountable for conversion and margin targets
- 5–8 years of experience

### Lead PM / AVP Product

- Leads multiple PMs across a vertical (trains, flights, buses) or platform (AI, payments)
- Works closely with business heads and group leadership
- 8–12 years of experience

### Director / VP Product

- Owns product strategy for a major area
- Partners with founders and leadership on company direction
- 12+ years of experience

- These ranges are estimates anchored on AmbitionBox data and adjusted for 2026.
- ESOP values depend on grant size, vesting and share price, and are not included in totals.
- AI-focused roles may be paid at the top of the band or above, given demand for these skills.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Typical progression:

1. APM → PM (1.5–2 years)
2. PM → Senior PM (2–3 years)
3. Senior PM → Lead PM / AVP (3–4 years)
4. Lead PM → Director (3–5 years)

Salary growth:

- Annual increments typically range from 8–15%
- Promotions usually bring 15–25% increases
- Lean teams mean PMs often get broad ownership early, which can accelerate promotion

- Stock options can add meaningfully to long-term earnings if the share price rises.
- Moves across group brands (ixigo, ConfirmTkt, AbhiBus) can broaden experience.

## Negotiating Your ixigo PM Offer

1. Benchmark carefully: Compare with Cleartrip, MakeMyTrip, and other Gurugram and Bengaluru consumer internet companies.
2. Clarify the level: Title and level set your band.
3. Focus on fixed pay: It compounds through increments.
4. Understand ESOPs: Ask about grant size, strike price, vesting and refresh practice. Since ixigo is listed, you can value options against the market price.
5. Ask for a sign-on bonus: Use it to offset forfeited bonuses or stock.

Negotiable items:

- Fixed pay within the band
- ESOP grant size
- Sign-on bonus
- Joining date and relocation support

Mistakes to avoid:

- Ignoring ESOP terms because they look small next to cash
- Comparing ixigo offers only with the largest companies' pay
- Not asking how bonuses are calculated

## FAQs

### What is the starting salary for an APM at ixigo?

Associate PMs can expect total cash compensation of around ₹12–20 lakh per year in 2026, depending on background and school. Candidates with strong data or engineering skills may land at the higher end.

### Does ixigo offer stock options to PMs?

ixigo uses employee stock option schemes. Grants are more common and larger at Senior PM and above, but some PM-level offers include them. Confirm the details in your offer letter.

### How does ixigo pay compare with Cleartrip and MakeMyTrip?

ixigo is broadly comparable at junior levels and may be lower at senior levels than MakeMyTrip or Flipkart-owned Cleartrip. Its advantages are stock options in a profitable listed company and broad ownership in lean teams.

### Do AI product roles pay more at ixigo?

Roles working on TARA, personalisation and agentic flows are in demand, and offers for experienced AI PMs may sit at the top of the band or above. Demonstrated experience shipping LLM-based products is the key differentiator.
