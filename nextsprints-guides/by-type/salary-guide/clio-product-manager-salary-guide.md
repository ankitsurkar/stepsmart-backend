## Clio Product Manager Salary Overview

As of 2026, Clio's Product Manager salaries remain competitive within the legal technology sector, reflecting the company's commitment to attracting and retaining top talent. Figures in this guide are in Canadian dollars unless noted. Clio's recent Canadian job postings list base pay of roughly $110,000-$150,000 for Product Managers, $138,000-$186,000 for Senior PMs, and up to about $233,000 for Staff PMs, with additional compensation in the form of bonuses and equity grants. Clio states it aims to hire between the minimum and the midpoint of each posted band. These are 2026 estimates based on Clio's pay-transparency job postings as listed on Built In and other job boards.

Clio has also changed materially since 2024: in November 2025 it completed its roughly US$1 billion acquisition of vLex and closed a US$500 million Series G at a US$5 billion valuation, up from US$3 billion in 2024, bringing headcount to about 2,000.

Several factors influence a PM's salary at Clio:

1. Experience: Years in product management and specific expertise in legal tech.
2. Performance: Individual and team contributions to product success.
3. Location: Salaries are adjusted based on the cost of living in different regions.
4. Education: Advanced degrees or relevant certifications may impact compensation.
5. Specialization: Expertise in areas like AI, data analytics, or user experience design.

Compared to industry standards, Clio's PM compensation package is generally on par with or slightly above average for similarly sized tech companies. However, Clio's strong focus on work-life balance and professional development often gives it an edge in overall employee satisfaction.

## Understanding Clio's PM Compensation Structure

Clio's Product Manager compensation package is designed to be comprehensive and competitive, consisting of several key components:

1. Base Salary: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, typically ranging from about C$110,000 to C$230,000+ depending on the PM's level and experience (more at director level).
2. Annual Bonus: Clio offers a performance-based bonus program, usually ranging from 10% to 25% of the base salary. Bonuses are tied to individual, team, and company performance metrics.
3. Equity Compensation: Stock options or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are a significant part of Clio's long-term incentive plan. The amount of equity granted increases with seniority and can substantially boost overall compensation.
4. Benefits and Perks: Clio provides a robust benefits package including: Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance Flexible work arrangements and unlimited vacation policy Professional development budget Wellness programs and mental health support 401(k) matching (for U.S. employees) or equivalent retirement plans

Here's how each component works:

- Base Salary: Reviewed annually, with adjustments based on performance and market conditions.
- Annual Bonus: Calculated at the end of each fiscal year, paid out in Q1 of the following year.
- Equity: Typically vests over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff, incentivizing long-term commitment.
- Benefits: Immediately available upon joining, with some programs having short waiting periods.

This structure is designed to align PM compensation with both short-term performance and long-term company success, fostering a sense of ownership among Product Managers.

## How Location Affects Clio PM Salaries

As a company with a distributed workforce, Clio adjusts salaries based on the cost of living in different locations. Here's how location impacts PM compensation:

1. Canadian roles (Clio is headquartered in Burnaby, British Columbia, with offices including Vancouver and Toronto): Postings carry CAD bands, and the bands above are based on them.
2. US roles: Clio also posts US-based PM roles, which carry separate USD pay bands that typically sit higher than Canadian bands.
3. Remote Work: Many PM roles are posted as remote within Canada (some excluding Quebec), with pay based on the local market.
4. International PM Compensation: For international hires, Clio benchmarks against local market rates while ensuring internal equity. Benefits packages are tailored to comply with local regulations and norms.

Clio's location-based compensation strategy aims to provide fair and competitive salaries while accounting for varying living costs and local market conditions.

## FAQs

### How often does Clio review and adjust its PM compensation structure?

Clio typically conducts annual compensation reviews to ensure its packages remain competitive. However, the company also monitors industry trends and may make adjustments more frequently if significant market shifts occur. These reviews consider factors such as industry benchmarks, cost of living changes, and Clio's overall performance.

### What unique benefits does Clio offer to Product Managers?

In addition to standard benefits, Clio offers several unique perks for PMs:

- Professional development support for conferences and workshops
- Access to legal industry events and networking opportunities
- Opportunities to work on legal tech products spanning practice management, payments, and AI

### How does Clio support work-life balance for its Product Managers?

Clio is known for its strong emphasis on work-life balance. Some key initiatives include:

- Flexible work hours and remote work options
- Unlimited vacation policy with a minimum vacation expectation to ensure PMs take time off
- Wellness programs including mental health support

### Can Product Managers at Clio transition to other roles within the company?

Yes, Clio encourages internal mobility. Product Managers often have opportunities to:

- Move between different product teams to gain diverse experience
- Transition into related roles such as Product Marketing or Product Operations
- Take on leadership positions in other departments leveraging their product knowledge
- Participate in cross-functional projects to expand their skill set

Clio supports these transitions through internal job postings, skills development programs, and cross-departmental mentorship opportunities.
