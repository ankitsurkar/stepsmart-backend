## Introduction

Atlan, the active metadata and data governance platform, is one of the highest-paying Indian-founded SaaS employers for product managers. It sells mainly to enterprise customers in the US and Europe, raised a $105 million Series C in 2024 at a reported $750 million valuation, and hires PMs across India (largely remote) and in the US. Its compensation reflects global SaaS norms: strong base pay plus stock options.

This guide covers 2026 PM salary ranges by level in INR for India and USD for US roles, how compensation is structured, how location affects pay, career progression and negotiation. Figures are estimates anchored on Levels.fyi, job postings and other public sources; treat them as ranges.

## Atlan Product Manager Salary Overview

In 2026, Atlan PM total compensation in India ranges from roughly ₹22 lakh for associate PMs to well above ₹1.5 crore for product directors. In the US, base salaries range from around $100,000 for associate-level roles to $300,000 for directors, before equity.

Public data points:

- Levels.fyi: Atlan Product Manager total compensation in India of about ₹61.5–85.7 lakh per year (base, stock and bonus).
- US job postings: Product Manager base pay listed at $149,000–199,500; Senior Product Manager (Data Quality) at $155,000–223,100.
- Comparably estimates (US, lower confidence): Product Manager around $106,000 and Senior PM around $147,000.
- AmbitionBox reviewers rate Atlan's salary and benefits around 4.1 out of 5.

Factors influencing salary:

- Level and scope (product area versus platform).
- Location (India versus US).
- Domain depth in data infrastructure and AI.
- Equity grant size and negotiation.

Compared with the market, Atlan's India pay for PMs is in the top tier of Indian SaaS and comparable to many global tech companies' India centres; US pay is in line with venture-backed enterprise SaaS.

## Understanding Atlan's PM Compensation Structure

1. Base Salary: The main component; reviewed annually. India packages include statutory benefits such as provident fund.
2. Equity (Stock Options): A meaningful share of total compensation, typically vesting over four years with a one-year cliff (standard for venture-backed startups; confirm details). Value depends on Atlan's future valuation and any liquidity events.
3. Performance Bonus: Less central than at large companies; some roles include variable pay.
4. Benefits: Health insurance, learning budgets, remote work support. Flexible, remote-first working for many roles.

| Component | Share of total (typical) |
| --- | --- |
| Base | 70–85% |
| Equity (annualised) | 15–30% |
| Bonus | 0–10% |

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Typical path:

1. Associate PM → PM: 1.5–2 years.
2. PM → Senior PM: 2–3 years.
3. Senior PM → Staff/Principal PM or Group PM: 2–4 years.
4. Director roles: depends on growth and openings.

Salary growth:

- Annual revisions based on performance and market.
- Promotions bring larger jumps plus refresh equity grants.
- Equity value grows if Atlan's valuation rises.

Growth tips:

- Become the go-to expert in one domain (lineage, AI context, governance).
- Build strong customer relationships; customer insight is highly valued.
- Write excellent PRDs; written communication carries weight in a remote culture.

### Understanding Your Equity

Key questions to ask before signing:

1. How many options, and what percentage of the company do they represent?
2. What is the strike price, and what valuation was used to set it?
3. What is the vesting schedule and cliff?
4. How long do you have to exercise after leaving?
5. Has the company run secondary sales or buybacks for employees, and could it again?

Even a rough answer to these questions helps you compare an Atlan offer fairly against one with liquid public stock.

## Negotiating Your Atlan PM Offer

1. Benchmark using Levels.fyi and peer offers from global SaaS companies' India centres and well-funded Indian SaaS firms.
2. Understand equity: number of options, strike price, latest valuation, vesting and exercise window.
3. Negotiate base first, then equity.
4. Use competing offers from data-infrastructure or SaaS companies.
5. Ask about refresh grants and promotion cycles.
6. Clarify remote expectations and travel.

Common mistakes:

- Valuing options at headline valuation without considering dilution and liquidity.
- Ignoring exercise windows if you leave.
- Accepting a lower level to speed up the process.
