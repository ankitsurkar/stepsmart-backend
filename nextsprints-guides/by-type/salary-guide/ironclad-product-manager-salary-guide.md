## Understanding Ironclad's PM Compensation Structure

Ironclad's product manager compensation package is designed to be competitive and align with the company's long-term success. The structure typically includes four main components:

1. **Base Salary**: This forms the foundation of the compensation package, providing a stable income that reflects the PM's experience and responsibilities.
2. **Annual Bonus**: Ironclad offers a performance-based bonus, which varies by level and is tied to both individual and company performance. Ironclad's job postings describe base salary as one part of a total rewards package that includes equity.
3. **Equity Compensation**: Stock options or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) are a significant part of the package, especially for more senior roles. The equity grant is usually vested over four years with a one-year cliff.
4. **Benefits and Perks**: Ironclad provides a comprehensive benefits package including: Health, dental, and vision insurance 401(k) matching Flexible PTO policy Professional development budget Remote work options Wellness programs

The base salary provides immediate compensation, while the bonus and equity components incentivize long-term performance and align the PM's interests with the company's success. The benefits package is designed to support work-life balance and personal growth.

3. **Senior PM**: Experienced PMs with 5-8 years in the field. Lead major product initiatives and mentor junior team members.
4. **Principal PM**: Top individual contributor role, typically with 8+ years of experience. Drive strategic product decisions and cross-functional leadership.

These ranges are guidelines and may vary based on individual performance, specific expertise, and market conditions. Equity grants, in particular, can have significant variability based on the company's valuation and growth trajectory.

## How Location Affects Ironclad PM Salaries

Ironclad, like many tech companies, has adopted a location-based pay structure that accounts for the cost of living and local market rates. Here's how location impacts PM salaries:

- **San Francisco Bay Area**: As Ironclad's headquarters location, this serves as the baseline for compensation. Salaries here are at the higher end of the ranges provided.
- **New York, Seattle, Los Angeles**: These tech hubs typically see salaries about 5-10% lower than the Bay Area.
- **Other Major U.S. Cities**: Depending on the local market, salaries may be 10-20% lower than the Bay Area benchmark.
- **Remote Work**: Ironclad offers remote work options with location-based adjustments. Remote workers in low-cost areas might see a 10-30% reduction compared to Bay Area salaries.
- **International**: For international PMs, Ironclad benchmarks against local market rates for similar roles in legal tech or enterprise software companies.

It's worth noting that Ironclad regularly reviews its location-based pay structure to ensure competitiveness in each market.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression at Ironclad follows a structured path, with opportunities for both vertical and horizontal growth:

1. **Vertical Progression**: PMs typically advance through the levels (Associate PM → PM → Senior PM → Principal PM → Director) as they gain experience and demonstrate impact.
2. **Horizontal Moves**: PMs can also expand their skills by moving between different product areas or taking on specialized roles (e.g., focusing on data products or enterprise solutions).

Salary growth correlates strongly with career progression:

- **Annual Increases**: PMs can expect annual salary increases of 3-5% for strong performance, even without a promotion.
- **Promotion Increases**: When moving to the next level, salary jumps are typically 15-25%.
- **Equity Refreshes**: High-performing PMs often receive additional equity grants to ensure long-term retention.

Timeframes for advancement vary, but as a general guideline:

- Associate PM to PM: 1-2 years
- PM to Senior PM: 2-3 years
- Senior PM to Principal PM: 3-4 years
- Principal PM to Director: 3-5 years (depending on business needs and individual readiness)

Ironclad emphasizes that these timeframes are not fixed, and exceptional performers may progress more quickly.

## Negotiating Your Ironclad PM Offer

When negotiating an offer with Ironclad, keep these tips in mind:

1. **Do Your Research**: Understand the market rates for your level and location. Use resources like Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, and your professional network.
2. **Focus on Total Compensation**: Don't fixate solely on base salary. Consider the value of equity, bonuses, and benefits in your negotiation.
3. **Highlight Your Unique Value**: Emphasize any specialized skills or experiences that align with Ironclad's needs, especially in legal tech or enterprise software.
4. **Be Transparent**: If you have competing offers, share them respectfully. This can give Ironclad a benchmark to work with.
5. **Consider Long-Term Growth**: Discuss career development opportunities and how Ironclad can support your professional goals.

Aspects typically open to negotiation include:

- Base salary (within the range for your level)
- Equity grants
- Sign-on bonuses
- Start date and relocation assistance (if applicable)

- Avoid making ultimatums or aggressive demands
- Remember that compensation is just one part of the overall opportunity at Ironclad
