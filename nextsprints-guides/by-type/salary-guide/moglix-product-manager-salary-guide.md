## Understanding Moglix's PM Compensation Structure

Moglix's compensation structure for Product Managers is designed to attract and retain top talent while aligning with the company's growth objectives. The package typically consists of:

1. Base Salary: This forms the core of the compensation, paid monthly and subject to annual revisions based on performance and market conditions.
2. Annual Bonus: Performance-linked bonuses are offered, usually ranging from 10-20% of the base salary, tied to individual and company performance metrics.
3. Equity Compensation: Moglix provides Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs) to PMs, with grant sizes varying based on seniority and potential impact. Vesting periods typically span 4 years with a one-year cliff.
4. Benefits and Perks: Health insurance for employees and dependents Life and disability insurance Flexible work arrangements Professional development allowances Paid time off and parental leave policies

The combination of these components aims to provide a balanced package that rewards performance and aligns PM interests with long-term company success.

## Moglix Product Manager Salaries by Level

Here's a detailed breakdown of salary ranges for each PM level at Moglix as of 2026:

| Level | Base Salary Range (₹ LPA) | Bonus (% of base) | Equity Grant (₹ value over 4 years) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Associate PM | 10-18 | 10-15% | 5-10 lakhs |
| Product Manager | 15-28 | 15-20% | 10-20 lakhs |
| Senior PM | 25-40 | 20-25% | 20-40 lakhs |
| Principal PM | 40-55 | 25-30% | 40-60 lakhs |
| Director of Product | 55-80+ | 30-40% | 60-100+ lakhs |

### Associate/Junior PM

Entry-level PMs typically have 0-2 years of experience. They focus on learning the ropes of product management and contributing to specific features or small products.

### Product Manager

Mid-level PMs with 2-5 years of experience take ownership of entire products or significant features, driving strategy and execution.

### Senior PM

With 5-8 years of experience, Senior PMs lead complex products or multiple product lines, influencing broader company strategy.

### Principal PM

Principal PMs have 8+ years of experience and are responsible for critical product areas, often managing other PMs and interfacing with executive leadership.

### Director of Product

Directors oversee entire product divisions, shape long-term product vision, and typically have 10+ years of experience in product management.

- These ranges are approximations and may vary based on individual performance, market conditions, and specific role requirements.
- Equity grants are subject to company valuation and may change with funding rounds or market conditions.

## How Location Affects Moglix PM Salaries

Location plays a significant role in determining PM salaries at Moglix:

- Major Tech Hubs: PMs in Bangalore, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai command the highest salaries, often 10-20% above the average range.
- Tier 2 Cities: Salaries in cities like Pune, Hyderabad, or Chennai may be 5-10% lower than in major hubs.
- Remote Work: As of 2026, Moglix has embraced a hybrid work model. Remote PMs are compensated based on their location, with adjustments made for cost of living differences.

International PM compensation at Moglix is still evolving as the company expands globally. PMs in Southeast Asian markets like Singapore may see packages adjusted for local market rates and cost of living.

## Career Progression and Salary Growth

Career progression for PMs at Moglix typically follows this path:

1. Associate PM → PM (2-3 years)
2. PM → Senior PM (3-4 years)
3. Senior PM → Principal PM (3-5 years)
4. Principal PM → Director of Product (4-6 years)

Salary growth is tied to these progressions, with significant jumps occurring with each promotion. On average, PMs can expect:

- A meaningful increase with each level advancement
- Annual raises within the same level, based on performance

Moglix also offers fast-track promotions for exceptional performers, potentially accelerating the typical timeframes.

## Negotiating Your Moglix PM Offer

When negotiating a PM offer at Moglix, consider these tips:

1. Research thoroughly: Understand market rates for your level and location.
2. Highlight your unique value: Emphasize skills or experiences particularly relevant to Moglix's B2B focus.
3. Consider the full package: Don't focus solely on base salary; equity can be a significant long-term value.
4. Be prepared to justify your ask: Use data and past performance metrics to support your desired compensation.

Aspects typically negotiable include:

- Base salary (within the range for your level)
- Equity grants
- Signing bonuses
- Flexible work arrangements

Common mistakes to avoid:

- Neglecting to negotiate at all
- Focusing only on short-term gains
- Failing to understand ESOP terms and how a possible IPO could affect their value
