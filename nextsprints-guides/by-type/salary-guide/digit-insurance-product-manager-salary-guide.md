## Introduction

Go Digit General Insurance, better known as Digit Insurance, hires product managers to build digital journeys for motor, health and travel insurance, partner platforms for agents and dealers, and claims technology. As a listed insurer since May 2024 with a technology-led brand, Digit sits between traditional insurers and consumer internet companies on pay. This guide gives 2026 estimates for Digit PM pay by level in INR, explains the pay structure and offers negotiation tips.

## Understanding Digit Insurance's PM Compensation Structure

**Fixed salary.** The main component. Digit pays competitively compared with traditional insurers because it competes with fintech and consumer tech for product talent.

**Annual performance bonus.** Typically 8–15% of fixed pay for mid-level roles and higher for senior roles, tied to company and individual performance. Insurance companies commonly link bonuses to profitability metrics.

**Stock-linked incentives.** As a listed company, Digit has employee stock option schemes. Senior PMs and leaders are more likely to receive grants. Because shares are listed, their value is easier to track than startup ESOPs.

**Benefits.** Health insurance for employees and family (naturally, from an insurer), group term life, leave policies and learning support.

Example package for a Product Manager with four years of experience:

- Fixed: ₹24 L
- Target bonus: ₹2.4 L (10%)
- Stock options: possible at this level, usually modest
- Total cash: around ₹26 L

## Digit Insurance Product Manager Salaries by Level

### Associate PM / Management Trainee

Entry-level product roles often come through campus management trainee programmes or analyst roles in product strategy. Pay of ₹10 L–₹20 L depending on school and experience. Associates own features such as a renewal reminder flow or a step in the partner app.

### Product Manager

Three to six years of experience. Total cash of about ₹20 L–₹32 L. PMs own a journey such as motor claims, travel purchase or POSP onboarding, working with engineering, underwriting and operations.

### Senior PM

Six to nine years. Around ₹32 L–₹48 L total cash. Senior PMs own a product line or platform, such as the partner platform or health claims technology, and influence pricing and distribution decisions.

### Lead PM / AVP Product

Nine to thirteen years. Around ₹45 L–₹65 L. They lead multiple PMs, set strategy for an area and work with business heads.

### VP / Head of Product

Senior leaders with thirteen or more years. Roughly ₹65 L–₹1.1 Cr in total cash plus stock incentives. They shape the company's digital product strategy.

## Negotiating Your Digit Insurance PM Offer

**1. Benchmark carefully.** Compare with insurtechs (Acko, Policybazaar), large private insurers (ICICI Lombard, HDFC ERGO) and fintechs. Insurers often pay slightly less in fixed pay than consumer tech but offer stability.

**2. Focus on fixed pay and bonus target.** Ask how bonuses have paid out in the last two years relative to target.

**3. Ask about stock options.** Understand eligibility, vesting and exercise price. Listed stock makes valuation transparent.

**4. Highlight relevant skills.** Experience with claims, pricing analytics, B2B2C platforms or regulated products can justify the top of a band.

**5. Discuss scope.** In a growing company, owning a high-impact area (health claims, partner platform) can accelerate promotion and pay growth.

## FAQs

### How does Digit's PM pay compare with fintech startups?

Digit's fixed pay is usually somewhat lower than well-funded fintech startups at the same level, but bonuses are more predictable and stock incentives are in a listed company. Total value depends on the stage and funding of the startup you compare with.

### Do Digit employees get stock options?

Digit has employee stock option schemes. Grants are more common at senior levels. Ask your recruiter about eligibility for your role.

### What do management trainees earn at Digit?

Estimates for campus management trainees range from about ₹10 L to ₹16 L per year depending on the campus and role, based on placement reports and salary aggregators.

### Are bonuses at Digit linked to company profitability?

Bonuses generally reflect both company and individual performance. As a listed insurer focused on profitable growth, company performance metrics are likely to play a significant role.
