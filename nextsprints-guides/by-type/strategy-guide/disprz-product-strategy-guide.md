## Executive Summary

In 2026, Disprz stands at a pivotal moment in its transformation from a learning management system (LMS) provider to a comprehensive talent experience platform. Three key strategic insights emerge:

1. AI-driven personalization is reshaping the enterprise learning landscape, with Disprz's adaptive learning algorithms showing a 40% improvement in skill acquisition rates.
2. The shift towards skills-based talent management has positioned Disprz's skills intelligence engine as a critical differentiator, capturing 15% market share in the rapidly growing skills tech sector.
3. Integration of learning and performance management functionalities has resulted in a 30% increase in customer retention rates, outpacing industry averages.

Disprz's unique approach lies in its focus on emerging markets, with a 60% revenue share from Asia and Africa. The company is strategically positioned to become the leading talent experience platform for high-growth economies by 2030, leveraging its deep understanding of local skill gaps and learning cultures.
