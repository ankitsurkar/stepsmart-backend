## Executive Summary

OLX is no longer one company. In India, OLX India has been part of CarTrade Tech since 2023 and has turned into a profitable, fast-growing classifieds business that sits beside CarWale and BikeWale. In Europe, OLX Group, owned by Prosus, has become a high-margin portfolio of horizontal and vertical marketplaces, reporting revenue of about US$992 million for the year to March 2026, up 28%, with adjusted EBITDA up 53%. Despite different owners, both businesses are making the same three strategic shifts:

1. **From listings to trust:** Verification, fraud detection and safer payments are becoming core products, not support functions. OLX India's AI-powered Verified User badge and Elite Buyer programme are examples.
2. **From horizontal to vertical depth:** Growth is concentrated in motors, real estate and jobs, where ticket sizes are large and professional customers pay for leads and tools.
3. **From search boxes to AI assistants:** OLX Group reports more than 85 AI use cases in production and a conversational car-search assistant on OTOMOTO that is also available inside ChatGPT. In India, CarTrade's AI tools such as VAYA and SuperDost route buyers and sellers across platforms.

For PM candidates, the key insight is that OLX's strategy is about monetising trust and intent more deeply, not about adding more listings.

## Introduction

Classifieds businesses are built on a simple loop: free listings bring buyers, buyers bring more sellers, and professional sellers pay for visibility. That loop made OLX a household name in India and across emerging markets. But the loop is under pressure. Facebook Marketplace offers free reach with social identity; specialist players such as CARS24, Spinny, Cashify and Vinted pick off high-value categories with managed transactions; and fraud undermines trust in peer-to-peer deals.

OLX has responded by narrowing and deepening. OLX Group exited or sold several markets, including India, and doubled down on Europe, while also acquiring La Centrale in France to strengthen motors. CarTrade bought OLX India for about ₹535 crore to combine its auto expertise with OLX's massive consumer traffic, which was around 68 million average monthly users at the time of the deal.

Key strategic questions:

1. How far should OLX move from lead generation into transactions such as payments, delivery and inspection?
2. How can OLX defend free horizontal categories while monetising verticals?
3. How should AI change discovery, listing creation and trust?
4. In India, how should OLX work with sister brands without cannibalising its own paying customers?

## OLX's Current Product Landscape

**OLX India (CarTrade Tech):**

- Horizontal classifieds app and website: mobiles, electronics, furniture, fashion, property, jobs and services.
- Cars and bikes: the most valuable categories, with dealer packages and private listings.
- OLX Autos C2B selling flows and remarketing linked to CarTrade's auction businesses.
- Monetisation: Featured ads, Boost to Top, Sell Faster packages and category packages.
- Trust: AI-powered Verified User badge, Elite Buyer, chat safety prompts.
- Partnerships reported in 2026 include used-car loans with a bank partner and quality-assured inventory from an organised used-car player.

**OLX Group (Prosus), Europe:**

- Horizontal marketplaces: OLX.pl, OLX.pt, OLX.ro, OLX.bg, OLX.ua.
- Motors: OTOMOTO, Standvirtual, Autovit, and La Centrale in France.
- Real estate: Otodom, Imovirtual, Storia.
- Jobs: OLX Praca and other local jobs sections.
- Transactions: OLX Delivery with buyer protection in Poland and other markets.

| Business | Main revenue | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OLX India horizontals | Paid visibility | Traffic and brand | Low monetisation per user, fraud perception |
| OLX India autos | Dealer packages, C2B | CarTrade infrastructure | Competition from managed players |
| OLX Europe verticals | Dealer, agent, employer subscriptions | Market leadership in several countries | Competition from portals and aggregators |
| OLX Europe horizontals | Visibility, delivery fees | Habit and scale | Vinted and Facebook in fashion and goods |

OLX Group's results show that growth comes primarily from verticals, where professional customers pay recurring fees, while horizontals act as traffic engines and trust platforms.

## Short-Term: The Next 12 Months

1. **Trust at scale (India)** Expand verification beyond paid badges, potentially offering basic verification free to raise the trust floor. Add chat-level fraud detection, warnings on advance-payment requests, and masked contact options. Success metric: Fraud reports per 1,000 chats down by a third.
2. **AI listing and search** AI-generated listings from photos and voice in Indian languages. Conversational search assistants rolled out from OTOMOTO to other vertical sites in Europe. Success metric: Listing completion rate and qualified leads per visitor.
3. **Dealer value proof** Lead-quality dashboards for car dealers in India and Europe. Clear placement rules when partner or assured inventory appears in search. Success metric: Dealer renewal rate.
4. **Delivery and payments in Europe** Increase delivery-enabled listings through better defaults and seller protection. Success metric: Shipped transactions per month.

> Interview Angle
> When asked about OLX's near-term priorities, lead with trust. Every other bet, from transactions to AI assistants, depends on buyers believing the person on the other side is genuine.

## Mid-Term: 1-5 Year Outlook

1. **Managed transactions in high-value categories:** Cars, bikes and phones in India are likely to see inspection, financing and protected payments, using CarTrade's dealer and auction infrastructure. Lower-value categories stay free and lightweight.
2. **Vertical SaaS for professionals:** Dealer management tools, agent CRMs and employer applicant tracking turn OLX verticals into workflow software, raising switching costs.
3. **Cross-platform identity and reputation:** A verified identity and transaction history that moves across OLX, CarWale and BikeWale in India, or across OLX Group's European brands.
4. **Embedded finance:** Car loans, insurance and buy-now-pay-later at the point of intent.
5. **Outcome-based pricing:** Pay-per-qualified-lead or pay-per-hire models in jobs and autos, made possible by better attribution.

Risks in this period include regulatory scrutiny of payments and data use, and channel conflict when platforms route leads between sister brands.

## Long-Term: 5-10 Year Projection

- **AI agents negotiating on behalf of users:** Buyers may describe what they want and let an assistant shortlist, negotiate and schedule. Marketplaces that own structured inventory and trust data will be the ones agents rely on.
- **Classifieds as infrastructure:** OLX could become an inventory and trust layer that powers other surfaces, including chat assistants such as ChatGPT, where OLX is already experimenting with distribution.
- **Circular economy positioning:** Rising interest in resale and sustainability may make used-goods platforms more central to consumer behaviour, especially in Europe.
- **Consolidation:** Both CarTrade in India and Prosus in Europe have shown appetite for acquisitions, and further consolidation of vertical portals is likely.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Make trust free, monetise convenience.** Basic verification should be universal; revenue should come from speed (boosts), certainty (inspection, protection) and tools for professionals.
2. **Adopt a two-speed product model.** Managed transactions for high-ticket categories, lightweight AI-assisted classifieds for the long tail.
3. **Protect paying professionals.** When introducing partner inventory or AI routing, guarantee transparent placement for dealers, agents and employers, or reprice packages.
4. **Build AI evaluation as a core capability.** With dozens of AI use cases live, OLX needs consistent ways to measure quality, fairness and cost.
5. **Design for India's diversity.** Indian-language interfaces, low-end devices and tier-2 cities should be first-class design targets.

| Recommendation | Impact | Difficulty | Time horizon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Universal basic verification | High | Medium | 12 months |
| Two-speed transactions model | High | High | 1–3 years |
| Professional placement guarantees | Medium | Low | 6 months |
| AI evaluation platform | Medium | Medium | 12 months |
| India-first design standards | Medium | Low | Ongoing |

## Key Takeaways

1. OLX India (CarTrade Tech) and OLX Group (Prosus) are separate businesses but share a strategy: trust, vertical depth and AI.
2. Verticals such as motors, real estate and jobs drive most growth and profit.
3. Trust products are moving from cost centres to revenue and retention drivers.
4. Transactions make sense where ticket sizes justify inspection, financing and protection.
5. AI is changing discovery and listing creation, and measuring AI quality is a new PM skill.
6. Channel conflict between sister brands and paying customers is a real risk that PMs must manage.

For PM interviews, show that you understand marketplace economics, both-sided trade-offs and the specific ownership structures behind each OLX business.
