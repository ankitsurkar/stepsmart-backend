## Executive Summary

In 2026, ixigo is turning a profitable, transport-led travel business into an AI-first, multi-category platform. Three shifts define its strategy:

1. **From trains to everything:** Trains built ixigo's audience; flights are now its largest vertical by gross transaction value (GTV), buses are growing fast, and hotels have become a reported segment.
2. **AI-native experience:** The May 2026 relaunch put TARA, a multimodal assistant in English, Hindi and Hinglish, at the centre of the app, along with Trip Mode and agentic flows that act on travellers' behalf.
3. **Capital for the next phase:** Prosus invested about ₹1,296 crore for a 10.1% stake in 2025, giving ixigo funds for AI, product and possible acquisitions.

ixigo reported Q1 FY27 GTV of about ₹5,524 crore (up 19%) and Q2 FY27 GTV of about ₹5,304 crore (up 22%), with profits in both quarters. Its strategic challenge is to keep growing profitably while competing with larger players in flights and hotels.

## Introduction

ixigo's 2026 launches signal where it is heading: an AI-native app with TARA, Trip Mode for the in-journey experience, agentic flows that send boarding passes to WhatsApp and share documents with Digi Yatra, and a separately reported hotels business that grew about 2.6 times year on year in Q2 FY27, including Brevistay's hourly stays.

The Indian travel market is growing, driven by rising air traffic, new regional airports, strong rail demand and more leisure travel from smaller cities. But it is competitive. MakeMyTrip leads in flights and hotels, Cleartrip uses Flipkart's reach and entered trains in 2026, redBus leads buses, and IRCTC dominates online train sales. ixigo faces three questions:

1. How can it grow flights and hotels without giving up the profitability that sets it apart?
2. Can AI create a real advantage, or will every app have similar assistants soon?
3. How should it manage a portfolio of brands: ixigo, ConfirmTkt and AbhiBus?

## ixigo's Current Product Landscape

Estimated GTV mix (based on company disclosures and analyst reports, approximate):

- Flights: about 40–45% (largest vertical by GTV in Q1 FY27)
- Trains: about 40% (down from more than half of GTV a few years ago, as other categories grow)
- Buses: about 12–15%
- Hotels: about 2%

Revenue mix differs from GTV: trains and buses earn relatively more revenue per rupee of GTV than flights, and ancillaries such as ixigo Assured add margin.

Key products:

- **ixigo Trains and ConfirmTkt:** Booking, PNR status, running status, waitlist predictions, alternatives, ixigo Assured free cancellation.
- **Flights:** Search and booking with fare alerts and ixigo Assured; Trip Mode for travel day.
- **AbhiBus:** Bus marketplace and the bus engine inside the ixigo app.
- **Hotels and Brevistay:** Budget and mid-range hotels, plus hourly stays.
- **TARA:** AI assistant for search, planning and service.

Recent wins and losses:

- **Win:** Flights grew 27% in Q1 FY27 and gained market share.
- **Watch:** Some analysts noted weakness in air in Q2 FY27, with hotels cushioning growth.

Strategic Position Matrix:

| High Market Share | Low Market Share |
| --- | --- |
| Private-app train bookings (ixigo + ConfirmTkt) | Hotels |
| Buses (with AbhiBus, among top players) | International flights |

## Short-Term: The Next 12 Months

ixigo's short-term priorities appear to follow three themes:

1. **AI adoption and conversion** Make TARA and Trip Mode useful for everyday tasks Fix performance on budget Android phones so AI features don't slow bookings Success metric: share of bookings influenced by TARA, with stable or rising conversion
2. **Flight share gains** Convert train users into flyers, especially in smaller cities Use ixigo Assured and Trip Mode to differentiate Success metric: flight GTV growth above market
3. **Hotels as a real business** Cross-sell hotels from train, bus and flight bookings Expand Brevistay short stays near transit hubs Success metric: hotels rising from about 2% of GTV

Strategic Dialogue Section: Three questions come up often in analyst and industry discussions:

1. Can ixigo win in flights against MakeMyTrip? Its edge is its train audience: millions of users who can be converted into first-time flyers at low acquisition cost.
2. Is AI a moat? Not by itself. The moat is AI combined with ixigo's data on Indian rail and bus travel and its vernacular user base.
3. Will hotels dilute focus? Only if ixigo tries to copy hotel-first competitors. Trip-linked and short stays play to its strengths.

## Mid-Term: 1-5 Year Outlook

ixigo's mid-term strategy is likely to rest on three bets:

1. **Agentic travel:** TARA moves from answering questions to completing tasks: rebooking cancelled flights, finding backups for waitlisted trains, confirming hotel rooms.
2. **Multi-modal trips:** Combined journeys (train plus bus, flight plus hotel) planned and booked in one flow, especially for pilgrimage and smaller-city destinations.
3. **Portfolio clarity:** ixigo as the full travel brand, with ConfirmTkt and AbhiBus as focused brands on a shared platform.

Market evolution to watch:

- IRCTC rule changes (for example tighter authentication for Tatkal) can reshape train funnels quickly.
- Airlines will push direct booking and new distribution standards.
- AI assistants from big tech may become a new channel for travel bookings.

| Bet | Expected Impact | Main Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agentic travel | Lower support costs, higher loyalty | Errors that cost users money |
| Multi-modal trips | Higher revenue per trip | Complexity and partner integrations |
| Portfolio clarity | Efficiency, cross-sell | Losing loyal ConfirmTkt or AbhiBus users |

## Long-Term: 5-10 Year Projection

Over the longer term, ixigo's position depends on:

1. **The next 300 million travellers:** As incomes rise in smaller cities, many people will take their first flight and first hotel stay. ixigo's train base gives it a head start in reaching them.
2. **AI as the interface:** If travel booking shifts to conversational assistants, the winners will be those with the best data, inventory and service operations.
3. **Consolidation:** The Indian online travel market may consolidate further. ixigo's profitability and Prosus backing give it options to acquire.

Potential long-term moves:

- Become the default travel app for Bharat, across all modes and stays.
- Offer TARA's travel intelligence to partners (B2B) or through other AI platforms.
- Expand into adjacent services like travel insurance, visa help and packages.

## Strategic Recommendations

For ixigo's leadership:

1. **Keep profitability as a product principle.** Growth should come from cross-sell and AI-led efficiency, not discount wars.
2. **Invest in trust for AI.** Clear consent, reversibility and accuracy for agentic features.
3. **Build hotels around trips,** not as a standalone hotel app.

For product managers at ixigo:

1. Design for budget phones and vernacular users first.
2. Measure AI features by outcomes (bookings, resolution time), not novelty.
3. Treat IRCTC and airline rules as product constraints to plan for.

For candidates preparing for ixigo interviews:

1. Understand the business: GTV, take rates, and why trains and buses matter.
2. Try TARA, Trip Mode and ConfirmTkt, and form opinions.
3. Prepare to discuss AI trade-offs: autonomy vs control, speed vs accuracy.

| Recommendation | Time Horizon | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TARA performance and accuracy on budget phones | 0–6 months | AI and platform |
| Train-to-flight conversion | 0–12 months | Trains and flights |
| Trip-linked hotels and Brevistay | 0–18 months | Hotels |
| Agentic disruption handling | 6–24 months | AI and flights |

## Key Takeaways

1. ixigo is profitable and growing, with flights now its largest vertical by GTV.
2. Its train audience is its unique advantage for growing flights and hotels.
3. The AI-native relaunch is a big bet; its success depends on accuracy, speed and trust.
4. Hotels are small but growing fast and could become a fourth growth engine.
5. Managing multiple brands on a shared platform is an ongoing strategic choice.

Bottom Line: ixigo's path forward is to turn its trusted train relationship into a full travel relationship, using AI to make every trip easier while keeping the frugal, profitable model that makes it different.
