## Executive Summary

In 2026, Scalable Capital stands at the forefront of Europe's digital wealth management revolution, having successfully navigated the transition from robo-advisor to comprehensive financial platform. With assets under management surpassing €10 billion and a client base exceeding 500,000 across Europe, Scalable Capital has emerged as a formidable challenger to traditional banks and investment firms.

Three key strategic insights define Scalable Capital's trajectory:

1. Hybrid advisory model: Blending AI-driven automation with human expertise to capture both tech-savvy millennials and high-net-worth individuals.
2. Expansion beyond ETFs: Diversifying into cryptocurrencies, private equity, and sustainable investments to meet evolving investor demands.
3. Pan-European growth: Leveraging regulatory harmonization to rapidly scale across EU markets, with a focus on France, Italy, and the Nordics.

Scalable Capital's market position has strengthened, now commanding 15% of the European digital wealth management market. The company's strategic direction is clear: to become the go-to financial super-app for European investors, offering a seamless blend of cutting-edge technology and personalized financial guidance.

## Scalable Capital's Current Product Landscape

Scalable Capital's product portfolio has evolved significantly since its inception, now encompassing a range of services that cater to diverse investor needs:

1. Robo-Advisory (40% of revenue): The company's core automated investment service.
2. Brokerage Platform (35% of revenue): A self-directed trading platform for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies.
3. Wealth Management (20% of revenue): Personalized advisory services for high-net-worth clients.
4. B2B Solutions (5% of revenue): White-label technology for banks and financial institutions.

In terms of market share, Scalable Capital has secured a strong position:

- 15% share of the European digital wealth management market
- 25% share in Germany, its home market
- 10% share in the UK, facing stiff competition from Nutmeg and Moneyfarm

Recent win/loss analysis reveals:

- Win: Successfully onboarded 50,000 new clients through its partnership with Barclays in Germany.
- Loss: Failed to gain significant traction in Spain due to strong local competition from BBVA's robo-advisory service.

Scalable Capital's short-term strategy revolves around three key themes:

1. Enhanced Personalization Launch of AI-driven investment themes based on individual preferences and risk profiles Implementation of natural language processing for more intuitive customer interactions Success Metric: 30% increase in user engagement with personalized features
2. Expansion of Asset Classes Introduction of fractional shares trading to lower entry barriers Integration of sustainable investment options across all product lines Success Metric: 25% growth in assets under management from new asset classes
3. Regulatory Compliance and Market Expansion Obtaining necessary licenses for operations in France and Italy Strengthening anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) processes Success Metric: Successful launch in two new European markets

## Mid-Term: 1-5 Year Outlook

Scalable Capital's mid-term strategy focuses on solidifying its position as a comprehensive financial platform:

Key Strategic Bets:

1. Development of a financial super-app integrating banking, investing, and financial planning
2. Expansion into adjacent services such as insurance and mortgage brokerage
3. Aggressive push into sustainable and impact investing

Build vs. Buy Decisions:

- Build: Enhancing core AI and machine learning capabilities for investment management
- Buy: Acquiring a neobank to fast-track the development of banking services
- Partner: Collaborating with established insurers to offer tailored products

Potential Market Entries/Exits:

- Entry: Nordic countries (Sweden, Denmark) leveraging EU passporting rights
- Exit: Potential divestment of B2B white-label solutions to focus on core consumer offerings

Strategic Framework Analysis: "Using the Strategy Triangle framework:

📌 Where to Play: Scalable Capital is targeting tech-savvy millennials and Gen Z across Europe, as well as affluent professionals seeking comprehensive financial services.

📌 How to Win: By offering a seamless, AI-driven financial ecosystem that combines the convenience of digital platforms with the trust and expertise associated with traditional financial institutions.

📌 Why Now: The convergence of open banking regulations, increased consumer trust in digital financial services, and the growing demand for sustainable investment options creates a unique opportunity for Scalable Capital to establish itself as the go-to financial platform for the next generation of European investors."

## Long-Term: 5-10 Year Projection

Scalable Capital's long-term vision is built on several core assumptions about market evolution:

1. Democratization of Private Markets: Increased retail investor access to private equity and venture capital investments.
2. AI-Driven Hyper-Personalization: Investment strategies tailored to individual life events, goals, and real-time financial situations.
3. Blockchain Integration: Adoption of blockchain technology for more efficient, transparent, and secure financial transactions.
4. Regulatory Harmonization: Further alignment of financial regulations across the EU, facilitating easier cross-border operations.

Major Technology Bets:

- Quantum Computing: Exploring applications in portfolio optimization and risk management
- Advanced Natural Language Processing: Developing conversational AI for more intuitive financial guidance
- Augmented Reality: Creating immersive financial planning and education experiences

Potential Disruption Factors:

- Decentralized Finance (DeFi): The rise of blockchain-based financial services could challenge traditional wealth management models
- Big Tech Entry: Companies like Google or Amazon launching comprehensive financial services platforms
- Climate Change: Shifting investment priorities and risk models due to environmental factors
