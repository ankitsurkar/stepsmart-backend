## Mid-Term: 1-5 Year Outlook

TechStyle Fashion Group's mid-term strategy focuses on several key strategic bets:

1. AI-First Product Development: Investing heavily in AI-driven design and trend forecasting to reduce time-to-market and improve hit rates.
2. Vertical Integration: Building out in-house manufacturing capabilities for key product lines to increase control over the supply chain and improve sustainability.
3. Global Expansion: Targeting emerging markets in Southeast Asia and Latin America with localized versions of its most successful brands.

Build vs. Buy Decisions:

- Build: Advanced AI and machine learning capabilities for personalization and design
- Buy: Sustainable material technology companies to accelerate eco-friendly product development

Potential Market Entries/Exits:

- Exit: Gradual phase-out of fast-fashion elements from JustFab to align with sustainability goals
