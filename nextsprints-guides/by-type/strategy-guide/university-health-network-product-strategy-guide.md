## Executive Summary

In 2026, University Health Network (UHN) stands at the forefront of digital health transformation, leveraging its unique position as Canada's largest research hospital network. Three key strategic insights emerge:

1. UHN's integrated data platform has become a cornerstone for personalized medicine, with over 1 million patient records driving AI-powered diagnostics.
2. Virtual care adoption has surged, with 40% of outpatient visits now conducted remotely, reducing wait times by 30%.
3. UHN's commercialization arm has successfully spun out five health tech startups, generating $100 million in additional revenue.

UHN now ranks as the #1 academic health sciences center in North America for research output and clinical impact. As healthcare shifts towards preventative, personalized, and remote models, UHN is strategically positioned to lead this evolution through its unique integration of research, education, and clinical excellence.
