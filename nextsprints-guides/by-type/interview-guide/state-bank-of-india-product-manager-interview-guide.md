## Introduction

State Bank of India is not the first name most students think of when they hear "product manager". Yet SBI runs some of the largest digital products in India. YONO has more than 10 crore registered users, YONO 2.0 launched in December 2025 with a goal of reaching 20 crore users in two years, and around two-thirds of new SBI savings accounts are now opened digitally. Around YONO sit YONO Business for corporates and MSMEs, BHIM SBI Pay for UPI, and group companies such as SBI Card, SBI Life, SBI Mutual Fund and SBICAP Securities, each with its own digital teams.

Product roles at SBI look different from those at a startup or a private bank. Most are filled through the bank's Specialist Cadre Officer (SCO) recruitment, either as regular officers in grades such as Deputy Manager (MMGS-II) and Manager (MMGS-III), or on contract at levels such as Assistant Vice President and Deputy Vice President. Selection is usually shortlisting followed by an interview, not a multi-round product loop. Group companies such as SBI Card hire more like private firms.

This guide explains what product roles at SBI involve, who gets hired, how the selection process works, what pay looks like, and how to prepare. It is written for students and early-career professionals who want to build products used by hundreds of millions of Indians.

> Who this guide is for
> Students and early-career professionals targeting Deputy Manager or Manager (Products – Digital Platforms) roles at SBI, digital product roles in SBI's Global IT Centre and Digital Banking teams, and product roles at SBI group companies such as SBI Card.

## PM Role

### Where product managers sit

- **Digital Banking (YONO and internet banking):** Owns YONO retail journeys such as onboarding, payments, pre-approved loans, the July 2026 Financial Fitness dashboard and the 3-in-1 savings, demat and trading account.
- **YONO Business and transaction banking:** Corporate and MSME journeys including bulk payments, approvals and, since July 2026, mobile trade finance and the YONO Ji AI assistant.
- **Payments:** UPI (BHIM SBI Pay and UPI inside YONO), cards, merchant acquiring and new rails.
- **Global IT Centre (GITC), Navi Mumbai:** Technology teams that build and run core platforms; product-minded officers work closely with them.
- **Group companies:** SBI Card, SBI Life, SBI MF and SBICAP Securities run their own product teams.

### What the job involves

The 2025 SCO notification for Manager and Deputy Manager (Products – Digital Platforms) describes work that will be familiar from any product role:

- Owning a product or journey end to end: requirements, prioritisation, launch and performance.
- Working with business units, IT, vendors, risk and compliance.
- Writing user stories and working in Agile teams; an older SBI product manager notice explicitly asked for product owner experience and knowledge of Agile.
- Tracking adoption and business outcomes, and fixing customer issues.

What makes SBI different is scale and context:

- **Scale:** A small change in YONO can affect crores of users. Peak loads on salary days and month-end are huge.
- **Inclusion:** Customers range from urban professionals to first-time rural users with DBT accounts.
- **Governance:** Every change passes through risk, compliance, IT security and often RBI guidelines.
- **Vendors:** Many systems are built or run by technology partners, so PMs manage partners as much as in-house engineers.

### A typical week

- Review YONO funnel dashboards, complaints and app ratings.
- Run a backlog grooming session with an Agile squad and vendor engineers.
- Meet the risk team about a change to transaction limits.
- Present a business case for a new journey to a General Manager.
- Join a migration war room as more users move to YONO 2.0.

## Job Requirements

### Eligibility (regular SCO roles)

Requirements vary by notification, but recent product roles have looked like this:

| Requirement | Deputy Manager (MMGS-II) | Manager (MMGS-III) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | B.E./B.Tech (CS, IT, Electronics etc.) or MCA; MBA often preferred | Same, MBA often preferred |
| Experience | Typically 2–4 years post-qualification in product, digital or fintech | Typically 5+ years, often with product ownership |
| Age | Roughly 25–32 | Roughly 28–35 |
| Selection | Shortlisting plus interview | Shortlisting plus interview |

The 2025 drive advertised 34 Manager and 25 Deputy Manager posts in Products – Digital Platforms. Always check the official notification on SBI's careers page for exact rules, since aggregator sites often disagree.

### Contractual roles

SBI also hires experienced specialists on 3-year contracts (often extendable by 2 years) at AVP, DVP and VP levels, with negotiable CTC ceilings. 2026 notifications for digital marketing and IT risk roles quoted ceilings of about ₹50 lakh for AVP and ₹60–63 lakh for DVP. These roles usually need 8–10+ years of experience.

### Skills interviewers look for

1. **Digital banking knowledge:** UPI, IMPS, NEFT, KYC and video KYC, Account Aggregator, card networks.
2. **Product fundamentals:** User journeys, prioritisation, metrics, Agile delivery.
3. **Regulatory awareness:** RBI digital lending rules, data protection, fraud prevention.
4. **Stakeholder management:** Working with branches, IT, vendors and senior management.
5. **Customer empathy across India:** Designing for both metros and villages.

> Expert Advice
> Bring concrete examples of shipping something in a regulated environment. Candidates report that interviewers respond well to stories about balancing speed with compliance, or about working with vendors to deliver on time.

## Interview Process Breakdown

SBI's process for regular specialist officer posts is more formal than a private company's, and decisions rest heavily on one interview.

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Online Application] --> B[Shortlisting]
    B --> C[Interview]
    C --> D[Merit List]
    D --> E[Medical and Background Checks]
    E --> F[Offer and Joining]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus Areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Application | Online on SBI careers portal | N/A | Eligibility, experience, documents |
| Shortlisting | Committee review | Several weeks | Relevance of experience |
| Interview | Panel, in person or video | 20–40 minutes | Domain, product thinking, motivation |
| Merit list | Based on interview marks | N/A | Ranking by category |
| Pre-joining | Medical, background checks | 2–6 weeks | Verification |

Detailed breakdown:

- Application and shortlisting:

Candidates apply online with details of education and experience. A committee shortlists based on how closely experience matches the role. Meeting minimum eligibility is not enough; relevant product and digital experience matters.

- Panel interview:

A panel of senior SBI officers, sometimes with external experts, conducts the interview. Reports for recent SCO drives describe it as carrying 100 marks, with final merit based on the interview. Expect questions on your past products, digital banking concepts, YONO, UPI, fraud, and how you would improve an SBI product.

- Contractual roles:

Contract hires typically go through shortlisting, interview and CTC negotiation.

- Group companies:

SBI Card and other group companies run private-sector-style processes: HR screen, two or three interviews with product and business leaders, and sometimes a case study.

Timeline expectations:

- From notification to final result: often 3–6 months for regular posts.
- Contractual and group company roles: usually 4–8 weeks.

## Product Manager Compensation & Levels at State Bank of India

SBI's regular officers are paid on public sector bank scales set through industry-level wage agreements, plus allowances and benefits. Contract roles have negotiated CTCs.

| Level | Typical product title | Approximate annual CTC (2026, estimate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MMGS-II | Deputy Manager (Products) | ₹18–24 lakh |
| MMGS-III | Manager (Products) | ₹24–32 lakh |
| SMGS-IV | Chief Manager | ₹30–38 lakh |
| Contract AVP | Assistant Vice President | Up to ~₹50 lakh |
| Contract DVP / VP | Deputy Vice President / Vice President | ₹55–90 lakh |

Beyond salary, regular officers get benefits such as subsidised housing loans, leased accommodation or house rent allowance, medical cover, pension and job security. These benefits are valuable but not always visible in CTC comparisons with private firms. See our salary guide for detail.

## How to Prepare

1. **Know SBI's digital products.** Use YONO 2.0, YONO Business (if you can), BHIM SBI Pay and the SBI Card app. Note what works and what frustrates users.
2. **Learn the basics of Indian digital banking.** UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, video KYC, Account Aggregator, credit on UPI, and RBI rules on digital lending.
3. **Read recent news.** YONO 2.0 launch and migration, the 20 crore user goal, the July 2026 Bank Day launches (Financial Fitness, 3-in-1 account, YONO Business trade finance, YONO Ji), and SBI Card's digital partnerships such as Google Pay Flex.
4. **Prepare product stories.** Two or three examples where you owned a product, used data to make decisions and handled stakeholders, ideally in a regulated setting.
5. **Practise product cases with an Indian lens.** For example: how would you get rural customers onto YONO, or why did digital account openings drop?
6. **Prepare for "why SBI?".** Talk about scale, inclusion and impact. Avoid sounding like you only want job security.

## FAQs

### Does SBI hire freshers into product roles?

Regular product roles under SCO recruitment usually require prior experience, typically two or more years for Deputy Manager. Freshers can join SBI as Probationary Officers through the PO exam and later move into digital teams, or join SBI group companies such as SBI Card, which hire some MBA graduates into product and digital roles.

### What kind of questions are asked in the SBI product interview?

Candidates report a mix of questions about their own experience, digital banking concepts and SBI's products. Expect questions such as "How would you improve YONO?", "How do you prevent UPI fraud?", "Explain video KYC" and "Describe a product you launched and its results." Some panels ask about general banking and current affairs.

### How technical do SBI product managers need to be?

Many product posts require an engineering or MCA degree. You should understand APIs, system integration, mobile app performance and security basics, because much of the work involves coordinating vendors and IT teams. You do not need to code in the interview.

### Is the selection only one interview?

For regular SCO posts, recent notifications describe shortlisting followed by an interview, with merit based on interview marks. Some posts in the past included written tests. Contract posts add CTC negotiation. Always read the specific notification.

### Where are SBI's digital product roles based?

Most are in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, at SBI's Corporate Centre and Global IT Centre. Some roles may be in other cities depending on the department. Group companies are mostly in Mumbai and Gurugram (SBI Card).

### How is working as a PM at SBI different from a fintech?

The scale and impact are much larger, but decisions take longer and pass through more approvals. PMs work more with vendors and governance processes. In return, they get stability, strong benefits, and the chance to shape banking for a huge share of India.
