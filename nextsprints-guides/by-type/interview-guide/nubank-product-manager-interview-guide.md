## Introduction

Nubank started in 2013 in a small house in São Paulo with one product: a purple, no-annual-fee credit card managed entirely through an app. Thirteen years later, Nu Holdings (NYSE: NU) serves about 139 million customers across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. In the second quarter of 2026 it reported revenue of nearly US$5.9 billion and its first quarter of more than US$1 billion in net income. It now operates as a bank in Mexico, has been obtaining a banking licence in Brazil, and in January 2026 received conditional approval from US regulators to form Nubank, N.A.

Product managers sit at the centre of this machine. Nubank's products include NuConta and Pix, the credit card, Ultravioleta, Caixinhas, NuInvest, Pix no Crédito, NuPay, personal and payroll loans, insurance, NuCel, Nu México and Nu Colombia, plus AI features such as its transaction foundation model nuFormer and an AI assistant used for Pix.

This guide explains the PM role at Nubank, what the company looks for, how the interview process is reported to work, how compensation is structured, and how students and early-career candidates can prepare.

> Insider Perspective
> Candidates often describe Nubank's process as structured and case-heavy, with recruiters sharing preparation material in advance. Interviewers look for customer obsession, clear thinking about trade-offs, and comfort working in English and Portuguese or Spanish.

## PM Role

> Nubank Product Manager
> A Nubank PM owns a product or part of a customer journey (for example, Pix, credit card limits, Caixinhas, NuPay or Nu México onboarding), working with engineering, design, data science, business analysts and risk to deliver customer and business outcomes.

Nubank organises teams around products and customer problems, often in squads with a PM, an engineering manager, engineers, a designer and analysts. PMs work across:

- **Banking and payments**: NuConta, Pix, tap-to-pay Pix, NuPay checkout.
- **Credit**: credit cards, limits, Pix no Crédito, personal loans, payroll loans.
- **Wealth**: Caixinhas, NuInvest.
- **Premium and lifestyle**: Ultravioleta, Nubank+, NuCel, NuTravel.
- **International**: Nu México (now a bank) and Nu Colombia; a US effort is being built.
- **Platform and AI**: data platforms, nuFormer, the AI assistant, fraud and security.

| Responsibility | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Customer research, data analysis, competitor analysis (Itaú, Mercado Pago, Inter, BBVA) |
| Prioritisation | Balancing customer value, risk, regulation and revenue |
| Execution | Writing specs, running experiments, shipping iteratively |
| Measurement | Metrics such as principality, delinquency, Pix usage and NPS |
| Collaboration | Working closely with risk, legal and compliance in a regulated environment |

Product roles are mainly in São Paulo (hybrid), Mexico City and Bogotá, with some roles in other locations including the US.

## Job Requirements

**Experience**

- Associate and early-career roles: 0–2 years, any degree, strong analytical skills. Nubank has run an Associate Product Manager programme in the past (a 12-month programme in São Paulo with two six-month rotations and a path to a PM role); check current early-career openings, since programme formats change.
- PM roles: typically 3+ years in product, consulting, banking or technology.
- Senior and Staff roles: 5–10 years, often with fintech, payments or credit experience.

**Skills**

- Analytical thinking: SQL, metrics, experiment design.
- Financial product understanding: credit risk, interest, interchange, deposits.
- Customer empathy for diverse users, from first-time credit customers to high-income Ultravioleta clients.
- Communication in English plus Portuguese (Brazil) or Spanish (Mexico, Colombia).
- Collaboration with risk, compliance and engineering.

**Values**

Nubank's stated values include customers loving them fiercely, challenging the status quo, being smartly efficient, acting like owners and building strong, diverse teams.

| Requirement | How to demonstrate it |
| --- | --- |
| Analytical skill | A project where data changed a decision |
| Customer obsession | Research you ran and what you learned |
| Financial understanding | A teardown of a Nubank product with economics |
| Language | Interview comfortably in English |

> Common Pitfalls
> Candidates often propose growth ideas without thinking about credit risk, fraud or regulation. At Nubank, a feature that increases usage but also increases defaults is not a success.

> Expert Advice
> Use the Nubank app deeply (or Nu México and Nu Colombia apps) and compare it with Itaú, Inter, Mercado Pago or BBVA. Bring specific observations to your interviews.

## Interview Process Breakdown

Based on candidate reports on Glassdoor and other sources, the process usually looks like this:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Application] --> B[Recruiter screen]
    B --> C[Hiring manager or PM call]
    C --> D[Product sense and execution cases]
    D --> E[Technical or analytical assessment]
    E --> F[Leadership and values]
    F --> G[Offer]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Video | 30 mins | Motivation, experience, English |
| PM call | Video | 45 mins | Past projects, problem solving, prioritisation |
| Product sense (often 2) | Video | 45–60 mins each | Design and improve products |
| Product execution (often 2) | Video | 45–60 mins each | Metrics, root cause, trade-offs |
| Technical assessment | Video | 45–60 mins | Data, systems, working with engineers |
| Leadership / values | Video or onsite | 45 mins | Ownership, collaboration, culture |
| Case presentation (some roles) | Video | 60 mins | Case study presented to senior PMs |

Detailed breakdown:

- Screening:

Recruiters assess motivation and communication, and some candidates report an English assessment early on.

- Product interviews:

a. Product sense: design or improve experiences, sometimes for non-Nubank products (a supermarket shopper experience is one reported example).

b. Product execution: measuring success of a feature, diagnosing metric drops, choosing between two equally important projects with limited resources.

c. Strategy and business: market entry, competitive response, pricing.

- Final rounds:

Leadership and values interviews, sometimes with a case presented to two senior PMs.

Timeline expectations:

- Usually 3–6 weeks.
- Recruiters often share preparation videos or documents.
- Some candidates report unclear feedback when rejected for seniority reasons.

## Product Manager Compensation & Levels at Nubank

Nubank uses an individual contributor (IC) ladder. Levels.fyi data for Greater São Paulo shows PM median total compensation of roughly R$333,000 at IC5, R$455,000 at IC6 and R$574,000 at IC7 per year.

Levels:

1. Associate PM / PM I (IC4)
2. Product Manager (IC5)
3. Senior Product Manager (IC6)
4. Staff / Lead PM (IC7)
5. Principal PM / Group PM (IC8+)

| Level | Estimated total compensation (BRL per year, 2026) |
| --- | --- |
| Associate PM (IC4) | R$180,000 – R$280,000 |
| Product Manager (IC5) | R$300,000 – R$400,000 |
| Senior PM (IC6) | R$400,000 – R$560,000 |
| Staff / Lead PM (IC7) | R$520,000 – R$720,000 |
| Principal / Group PM (IC8+) | R$750,000 – R$1,100,000+ |

Note: total compensation includes base salary (paid in Brazil with a 13th salary and vacation bonus), profit sharing (PLR) and restricted stock units in Nu Holdings. Ranges are estimates and vary by team and performance. Mexico roles are paid in MXN and US roles in USD.

## How to Prepare

**Know the products and the business**

- Use NuConta, Pix, the credit card, Caixinhas, NuInvest and, if possible, Nu México or Nu Colombia.
- Understand the business model: interchange, credit interest, deposits, fees, and very low cost to serve (efficiency ratio near 20%).
- Follow recent moves: Mexico banking licence, US charter process, Ultravioleta changes, NuPay partnerships, NuCel and AI initiatives.

**Practise fintech cases**

- Designing for financial inclusion (first credit card, first emergency fund).
- Metrics for payments and credit products.
- Root cause analysis for approval rates, Pix usage or delinquency.
- Strategy questions on Mexico, Colombia and the US.

Use NextSprints' Product Manager Interview Questions to practise Nubank-style cases with worked answers.

**Prepare your stories**

Use the STAR method to show ownership, customer obsession and data-driven decisions. Prepare examples of disagreements with stakeholders such as risk or legal teams.

**Practise in English**

Even in São Paulo, parts of the process are often in English. Practise explaining cases clearly in English.

**Mock interviews**

Practise with PMs from fintech or banking. NextSprints PM Coaching can connect you with experienced PMs for mock interviews.

## FAQs

### Does Nubank hire product managers from university?

Nubank has historically run an Associate Product Manager programme for early-career candidates from any degree, based in São Paulo. It also runs broader early-career programmes. Check Nubank's careers page and recruiting posts for current openings.

### Are interviews in English or Portuguese?

Many candidates report that at least part of the process is in English, and some are fully in English. Roles in Brazil also require Portuguese; Mexico and Colombia roles require Spanish.

### What kinds of cases are asked?

Reported cases include measuring the success of a feature, designing an improved experience for a shopper, and choosing between two equally prioritised projects with limited resources. Some candidates say cases are unrelated to Nubank's products, so practise general product sense as well as fintech cases.

### How important is knowledge of credit and banking?

Very helpful. Many Nubank products involve credit risk, fraud and regulation. You do not need to be a credit expert, but you should understand how lending makes and loses money.

### Can I join Nubank's Mexico or Colombia teams from Brazil?

Some roles allow transfers or regional work, but most require living in the relevant country. International experience and Spanish are strong advantages.
