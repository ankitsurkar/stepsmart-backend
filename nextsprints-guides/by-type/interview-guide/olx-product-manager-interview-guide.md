## Introduction

OLX is one of the best-known names in online classifieds. In India, OLX India is the go-to app for buying and selling used phones, cars, bikes, furniture and almost anything else, and since 2023 it has been part of CarTrade Tech, the listed company behind CarWale and BikeWale. Outside India, OLX Group, owned by Prosus, runs classifieds and vertical marketplaces in Europe, including OLX in Poland, Portugal, Romania, Bulgaria and Ukraine, and motors and real estate brands such as OTOMOTO, Otodom, Standvirtual, Imovirtual, Autovit and Storia. OLX Group reported revenue of about US$992 million for the year to March 2026, up 28%, with growth led by motors, real estate and jobs.

For students and early-career candidates, OLX is attractive because it is a genuinely two-sided marketplace business. PMs think about buyers and sellers at the same time, balance free supply against paid visibility, and fight fraud while trying to keep deals fast. That makes OLX interviews a strong test of marketplace thinking.

This guide covers both tracks most candidates encounter:

- **OLX India (CarTrade Tech), Gurugram:** Associate Product Manager, Product Manager and Senior PM roles in seller experience, buyer experience, trust and safety, monetisation and autos.
- **OLX Group Europe:** PM and Senior PM roles in Lisbon, Poznań, Warsaw, Bucharest, Barcelona and Berlin, often in motors, real estate, jobs, payments and delivery, martech and AI.

> Know which OLX you are interviewing with
> OLX India and OLX Group are now separate businesses with different owners. Read the job description carefully and tailor your examples: CarTrade's auto ecosystem in India, or Prosus-backed vertical marketplaces in Europe.

## PM Role

Product managers at OLX own a slice of a marketplace and are accountable for both sides of it.

**Typical product areas at OLX India:**

1. **Seller experience:** Listing creation, AI-assisted listing, pricing guidance, Mark as Sold and seller dashboards.
2. **Buyer experience:** Search, feed ranking, ad-detail pages and chat.
3. **Trust and safety:** The AI-powered Verified User badge, fraud detection in chat, and programmes such as Elite Buyer.
4. **Monetisation:** Featured ads, Boost to Top, category packages and dealer subscriptions.
5. **Autos and transactions:** OLX Autos selling flows, inspection, dealer leads, and partnerships such as used-car loans.

**Typical product areas at OLX Group Europe:**

1. Motors (OTOMOTO, Standvirtual, Autovit) including conversational AI car search.
2. Real estate (Otodom, Imovirtual, Storia) including seeker experience and agent tools.
3. Jobs (for example OLX Praca in Poland).
4. Payments, wallet and delivery products, such as OLX Delivery in Poland.
5. Growth, martech and CRM.

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**

- Define problems with data from funnels, experiments and user research.
- Write product requirements and prioritise backlogs with engineering and design.
- Run A/B tests and interpret results, often with cluster or marketplace-level designs.
- Work with sales and category teams who talk to dealers, agents and employers.
- Balance trust and safety constraints with growth targets.

> Expert Advice
> Marketplace PMs are judged on liquidity. Learn to talk about supply, demand, match rate and time-to-transaction, and you will sound like an OLX PM before you are one.

## Job Requirements

**For APM and early-career PM roles (India):**

- 0–3 years of experience, often from engineering or MBA backgrounds; product analyst or business analyst experience helps.
- Strong analytical skills: SQL, Excel, funnel and cohort analysis.
- Clear structured thinking in product cases.
- Understanding of Indian consumer behaviour across metros and tier-2 cities.

**For PM and Senior PM roles:**

- 3–8+ years of product experience, ideally in consumer apps, marketplaces, e-commerce or fintech.
- Track record of shipping features that moved measurable metrics.
- Experience with experimentation and data-informed decisions.
- Stakeholder management with sales, operations and trust teams.

**For Europe roles:**

- Fluent English; Polish, Portuguese or Romanian is a plus for local-market roles.
- Experience with classifieds, motors, real estate or jobs is valued.
- Comfort working in distributed, cross-country teams.

| Skill | Why it matters at OLX | How to show it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace thinking | Every decision affects buyers and sellers | Discuss both sides in every case |
| Data fluency | Decisions rely on funnels and experiments | Bring a metric tree into your answers |
| Trust and safety judgment | Fraud is the biggest brand risk | Raise risks and guardrails unprompted |
| Monetisation sense | Revenue comes from paid visibility and dealers | Explain how a feature affects paid products |
| AI product sense | OLX is investing in AI listing, search and verification | Talk about evaluation, cost and failure modes |

## Interview Process Breakdown

Interview processes vary by team and country, and public reports are limited, so treat this as a typical pattern rather than a fixed script.

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Application or Referral] --> B[Recruiter Screen]
    B --> C[Hiring Manager Round]
    C --> D[Product Case Round]
    D --> E[Analytics or Assignment]
    E --> F[Leadership and Culture Round]
    F --> G[Offer]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus Areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Phone or video | 20–30 mins | Background, motivation, salary expectations, knowledge of OLX |
| Hiring manager round | Video | 45–60 mins | Past projects, product thinking, team fit |
| Product case | Video, sometimes on Miro | 60 mins | Product design, improvement or metrics case on an OLX product |
| Analytics or assignment | Take-home or live | 1–3 days or 60 mins | Data interpretation, business case, SQL-style reasoning |
| Leadership round | Video or on-site | 45 mins | Strategy, ownership, values; sometimes with the CPO or head of product |

**Detailed Breakdown:**

- Recruiter Screen:

Candidates report questions about why OLX, what they know about the company and their salary range. For Europe roles, some candidates say salary expectations come up early.

- Hiring Manager Round:

Expect a deep dive into one or two projects on your CV: the problem, the data, the decision and the outcome. Interviewers typically probe what you would do differently.

- Product Case:

Candidates for OLX Group roles in Lisbon have described a product case worked through on Miro with a senior PM and a designer, similar to standard PM prep book questions but with OLX-specific data to analyse. Typical prompts include improving chat, measuring a trust feature, or diagnosing a drop in leads.

- Analytics or Assignment:

Some candidates report a business case to prepare and present, for example a monetisation proposal for a category. India roles may include data interpretation or guesstimates.

- Leadership Round:

A conversation with senior product leadership about strategy, judgment and how you work with others.

Timeline expectations:

- Most processes take 2–5 weeks from first call to offer.
- Candidates have described some processes as quick and others as disorganised, so follow up politely if timelines slip.

## Product Manager Compensation & Levels at OLX

OLX India pays in INR, with fixed pay, a performance bonus and, for some senior roles, equity-linked incentives tied to CarTrade Tech. OLX Group pays in local currency in Europe, typically base salary plus an annual bonus (job posts commonly mention up to 15%).

## How to Prepare

**Know the products deeply:**

1. Use OLX India as both a buyer and a seller. Post a real item, note every step, and track how chat feels.
2. Explore paid products: Sell Faster packages, Featured ads and Boost to Top.
3. For Europe roles, explore OTOMOTO, Otodom or OLX.pl and try OLX Delivery.
4. Read CarTrade Tech's investor presentations or OLX Group's annual results for strategy context.

**Practise marketplace cases:**

- Design: a safe-deal flow for used phones, or a dealer-backed instant offer for car sellers.
- Improvement: OLX chat reply rates or OLX Delivery adoption.
- Metrics: measuring the Verified User badge or an AI car-search assistant.
- Root cause: a drop in chats per listing or dealer package renewals.
- Strategy and trade-offs: listing fees, phone number masking, moving from listings to transactions.

**Tailor your CV:** Quantify impact on supply, demand or conversion. Use the STAR method and highlight experiments you ran.

## FAQs

### Is OLX India still part of OLX Group?

No. CarTrade Tech agreed to acquire OLX India's classifieds and auto business for about ₹535 crore in 2023, so OLX India now operates within the CarTrade group alongside CarWale and BikeWale. OLX Group, owned by Prosus, continues to run OLX brands in Europe and other markets. Candidates should prepare for the specific company they are interviewing with.

### Does OLX hire freshers for product roles?

Openings for APMs and product analysts appear periodically at OLX India, and OLX has historically hired engineers through campus drives. Pure fresher PM roles are rare; many candidates move into product after one to three years in analytics, engineering or business roles, or after an MBA.

### What kind of case questions should I expect?

Expect marketplace cases rooted in real OLX products: improving chat, designing trust features, measuring paid products, or diagnosing a drop in leads. Interviewers typically look for both-sided thinking, data-driven metrics and awareness of fraud risks.

### Is there a take-home assignment?

Some teams use a business case or presentation, and some use a live data exercise. Candidates report that the case is often presented to the hiring manager or a panel, followed by discussion of assumptions and metrics.

### How important is AI experience for OLX PM roles?

Increasingly important. OLX Group reported investing about US$30 million in AI in its latest year and deploying dozens of AI use cases, while OLX India has launched AI-powered verification. You do not need to be an ML engineer, but you should be able to discuss how to evaluate an AI feature, its costs and its failure modes.
