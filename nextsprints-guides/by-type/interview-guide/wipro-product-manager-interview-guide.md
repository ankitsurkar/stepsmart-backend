## Introduction

Wipro is one of India's largest IT services companies, headquartered in Bengaluru, with roughly US$10.5 billion in annual revenue and about 2.3 lakh employees. Led by CEO Srini Pallia since April 2024, Wipro is repositioning around AI: the Wipro ai360 ecosystem (launched in 2023 with a US$1 billion investment commitment), the Wipro Intelligence suite of AI platforms (including WEGA and WINGS), the Lab45 AI platform, the FullStride Cloud business, and acquisitions such as HARMAN DTS, Mindsprint and Alpha Net in 2025–26.

For students, Wipro is best known for mass campus hiring through Elite NLTH and Turbo. But Wipro also hires product managers, associate product managers and product owners in several places:

- **AI and platform teams:** Lab45, Wipro Intelligence (WEGA, WINGS), legacy automation platforms such as Wipro HOLMES
- **Product businesses:** Topcoder (crowdsourcing marketplace) and Wipro Gallagher Solutions (NetOxygen mortgage loan origination for US lenders)
- **FullStride Cloud and engineering:** Cloud platforms, accelerators and engineering products
- **Client-facing digital programmes:** Product Owner roles on clients' agile teams in banking, retail, healthcare and energy
- **CIO function:** Internal products for Wipro's own employees and operations

> What makes Wipro different
> Candidates report that Wipro product interviews blend product thinking with client and delivery awareness. Interviewers typically want to see that you can design a good product and also explain how it would be delivered, governed and sold to enterprise clients.

## PM Role

> Wipro Product Manager
> A Wipro Product Manager or Product Owner defines and delivers AI platforms, software products or client digital products, balancing user needs, client commitments, delivery constraints and Wipro's commercial goals.

Responsibilities:

- Own the roadmap and backlog for a platform, product or client product area
- Translate client and user needs into epics, user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work with engineering, data science, design and delivery teams across India and global locations
- Support pre-sales: demos, solution walkthroughs and RFP responses
- Define and track success metrics such as adoption, productivity gains and client satisfaction
- Ensure responsible AI, security and compliance in AI-driven products

Where PMs sit:

| Area | Product focus | Typical PM work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lab45 and Wipro Intelligence | AI platforms, agents (WEGA, WINGS) | Agent features, governance, platform adoption |
| FullStride Cloud | Cloud accelerators, FinOps, managed cloud tooling | Productising cloud services |
| Topcoder | Crowdsourcing marketplace | Two-sided marketplace growth and quality |
| Wipro Gallagher Solutions | NetOxygen mortgage LOS | Vertical SaaS for US lenders |
| Client programmes | Banking, retail, healthcare products | Product Owner on agile teams |
| CIO function | Internal tools and platforms | Employee experience products |

Locations: Bengaluru (Sarjapur, Electronic City), Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Noida and Kolkata, plus roles in the US, UK and Europe.

## Job Requirements

Qualifications:

- Engineering or MBA degrees are common; domain degrees help for vertical products
- Lateral PM roles generally need 3–10 years of experience
- Agile certifications (CSPO, SAFe POPM) and AI or cloud certifications are valued

Skills:

| Skill | Why it matters at Wipro |
| --- | --- |
| AI and GenAI literacy | Most new products involve AI agents or platforms |
| Enterprise and client empathy | Products are sold to and used by large enterprises |
| Backlog and agile delivery | Most work runs in Scrum or SAFe |
| Data-driven decisions | Productivity and outcome metrics drive deals |
| Communication and stakeholder management | Clients, delivery leaders and global teams |
| Domain knowledge | Banking, insurance, healthcare or retail for vertical roles |

Experience level guide:

- **Campus (0–2 years):** Engineering or business analyst roles; product ownership later
- **Associate PM (1–5 years):** Feature ownership within a platform or product
- **PM / Product Owner (4–9 years):** Own a product area or client product backlog
- **Senior PM (8–14 years):** Own a product line, influence strategy and pre-sales
- **Product Lead / Head (12+ years):** Own portfolios and P&L

## Interview Process Breakdown

Lateral process as reported by candidates on Glassdoor, Prepfully, Interview Query and Board Infinity:

```mermaid

    A[Application or Referral] --> B[Online Assessment or Recruiter Screen]

    C --> D[Product or Client Round 2]
    D --> E[Managerial or Culture Fit]
    E --> F[HR]
    F --> G[Offer]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screening | Phone or online test | 20–60 mins | Background, aptitude, motivation |
| Technical and product round(s) | Video | 45–60 mins each | Product design, agile, metrics, AI awareness |
| Managerial / culture fit | Video or in person | 30–45 mins | Cross-functional leadership, client scenarios |
| HR | Phone or video | 20–30 mins | Compensation, notice, location |

Detailed breakdown:

- Technical and product rounds:

Candidates report product design prompts, agile scenarios and questions on managing cross-functional teams and client interactions. For AI platform roles, expect questions on how you would measure an AI agent's success or govern hundreds of agents.

- Managerial round:

Expect scenarios such as "A client wants a feature that breaks platform standards" or "Your agent's accuracy dropped after a model update." Interviewers typically assess structured thinking and transparent communication.

- Campus product interviews:

One campus candidate reported a single round: an introduction, a product design question and a few HR questions on why they wanted to be a PM at Wipro.

- HR round:

Standard discussion of CTC, variable pay, joining date and location.

Difficulty: Glassdoor reports place the process around 3 out of 5. Timeline: typically 2–4 weeks.

## How to Prepare

Preparation timeline:

1. **Week 1: Understand Wipro's AI and platform strategy.** Read about ai360, Wipro Intelligence (WEGA, WINGS), Lab45 and FullStride Cloud.
2. **Week 2: Practise product cases.** Design, improvement, metrics and RCA questions on AI agents, cloud services and marketplaces like Topcoder.
3. **Week 3: Enterprise and delivery thinking.** Learn how services contracts, SLAs and outcome-based pricing work.
4. **Week 4: Behavioural stories.** Prepare examples of stakeholder conflict, delivery under pressure and influencing clients.

Practice resources:

- Wipro press releases, annual reports and earnings call transcripts
- Analyst coverage of agentic services and IT services AI strategies
- NextSprints Wipro question bank

> Success tip
> When you propose a feature, also explain how it would be measured and governed for an enterprise client. Wipro's products are sold on trust and outcomes, so metrics and guardrails make your answers stand out.

## FAQs

### Does Wipro have a formal APM program?

Wipro does not run a widely known branded APM programme. Most graduates join through Elite NLTH, Turbo or MBA campus hiring in engineering, analyst or consulting roles, then move into product ownership. Lab45, Topcoder and platform teams sometimes hire associate PMs laterally.

### How technical are Wipro PM interviews?

Moderately technical. You should understand APIs, cloud basics and how AI models and agents work at a conceptual level. Coding is rarely required, but you may be asked how you would work with engineers on architecture trade-offs.

### What kind of product questions are asked?

Candidates report product design questions, agile and backlog scenarios, metrics questions and cross-functional leadership scenarios. AI-related questions, such as measuring an AI assistant's success, are increasingly common.

### How long does the Wipro PM interview process take?

Typically 2–4 weeks for lateral roles, though approvals and background checks can extend this. Campus processes are usually completed within a day or a few days.

### Can I move from a Wipro engineering role into product management?

Yes. Many product owners start as engineers or business analysts on client programmes. Building domain knowledge, taking ownership of requirements and joining AI platform initiatives are common paths into product roles.
