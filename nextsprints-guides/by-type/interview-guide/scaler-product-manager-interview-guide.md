## Introduction

Scaler is one of India's best-known technology education companies. Founded by Abhimanyu Saxena and Anshuman Singh, it grew out of InterviewBit, the coding interview practice platform they launched in the mid-2010s, and expanded into Scaler Academy for working software engineers, data science and AI programs, the free learning library Scaler Topics, and two residential schools: Scaler School of Technology (SST) for undergraduate computer science and, more recently, AI and Business, and Scaler School of Business. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Scaler reported revenue of about ₹363 crore in FY25 and cut its losses by roughly 98% to near break-even.

In 2026, Scaler repositioned itself as an AI-native technology career platform, rebuilding its flagship program as Modern Software & AI Engineering and launching new programs such as a PGP in Business and AI. For PM candidates, that makes Scaler an interesting place: products span free content, practice platforms, expensive cohort-based programs, AI learning tools and physical campuses, and every product is judged by one hard question: did learners actually get better careers?

This guide covers the PM roles Scaler hires for, what interviewers look for, how the process typically works, compensation, and how to prepare.

> Why Scaler is a distinctive PM environment
> Ed-tech PMs balance learning outcomes, learner experience and sales efficiency. At Scaler, you will also work closely with instructors, mentors and admissions teams, so cross-functional judgment matters as much as product sense.

## PM Role

Product managers at Scaler typically own one of these areas:

1. **Learning experience:** The platform learners use every day: live classes, recordings, assignments, contests, doubt resolution and progress tracking.
2. **AI learning products:** AI tutors, AI mock interviewers, personalised learning paths and evaluation tools.
3. **Free platforms and growth:** InterviewBit, Scaler Topics, masterclasses and the funnel from free learners to paid programs.
4. **Admissions and sales tools:** Lead management, counselling workflows, entrance tests and payment and financing flows.
5. **Careers:** Job boards, employer partnerships, resume tools and outcome tracking.
6. **Campus products:** Systems for Scaler School of Technology and Scaler School of Business students, faculty and parents.

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**

- Analyse learner funnels and engagement, and design experiments.
- Work with instructors and curriculum teams to turn learning goals into product features.
- Partner with admissions and marketing on conversion and lead quality.
- Write specifications and prioritise backlogs with engineering and design.
- Track outcome metrics such as completion, interview readiness and placements.

Candidates report that the role titles include Associate Product Manager, Product Manager, Product Manager II and Senior Product Manager, with product intern roles for students.

> Expert Advice
> Show you understand that ed-tech success is measured by learner outcomes, not just engagement. Interviewers respond well to candidates who question vanity metrics.

## Job Requirements

**For product interns and APMs:**

- Strong academic background, often engineering; coding familiarity is a plus because users are developers.
- Analytical skills: SQL, spreadsheets, funnel analysis.
- Structured problem solving and clear writing.
- Genuine interest in education and learning.

**For PM and PM II roles:**

- 2–6 years of product or related experience, ideally in consumer apps, ed-tech, SaaS or marketplaces.
- Experience with experimentation and growth funnels.
- Ability to work with non-product stakeholders such as instructors and sales teams.

**For Senior PM roles:**

- 6+ years of experience owning a product area end to end.
- Track record of measurable outcomes and leading cross-functional teams.

| Skill | Why it matters at Scaler | How to show it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning outcome thinking | Programs are judged by careers | Tie features to completion and outcomes |
| Funnel and growth analysis | Free-to-paid conversion drives revenue | Break down conversion steps |
| Empathy for developers | Learners are engineers and aspiring engineers | Use developer pain points in examples |
| AI product sense | Scaler is AI-native | Discuss evaluation, cost and failure modes of AI tutors |
| Stakeholder management | Instructors, mentors, sales | Show how you aligned different teams |

## Interview Process Breakdown

Public reports on Scaler's PM interviews are limited, so treat this as a typical pattern. One Glassdoor review for a Product Manager II role in Bengaluru described a process of around three weeks that included an assignment.

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Application or Referral] --> B[Recruiter Screen]
    B --> C[Assignment]
    C --> D[Product Case Interview]
    D --> E[Hiring Manager Round]
    E --> F[Founder or Leadership Round]
    F --> G[Offer]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus Areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Phone or video | 20–30 mins | Background, motivation, why Scaler |
| Assignment | Take-home | 2–4 days | Product problem on a Scaler product, often with data |
| Product case interview | Video | 45–60 mins | Discussion of assignment, product design or metrics case |
| Hiring manager round | Video | 45 mins | Past projects, execution, collaboration |
| Leadership round | Video or in person | 30–45 mins | Strategy, ownership, culture fit |

**Detailed Breakdown:**

- Recruiter Screen:

Expect questions about your interest in education and what you know about Scaler's products. Candidates in other roles report being asked what they know about Scaler, so research before the call.

- Assignment:

A take-home problem, such as improving retention on InterviewBit or measuring a program feature. Interviewers typically look for clear problem framing, data-informed prioritisation and realistic metrics.

- Product Case Interview:

A deep discussion of your assignment, plus a live case. Common themes: learner engagement, funnel conversion, AI tutor design and outcome metrics.

- Hiring Manager Round:

Focused on your past work and how you collaborate with engineers, designers and non-product teams.

- Leadership Round:

May involve senior leaders. Expect questions on Scaler's strategy, the future of tech careers in the AI era, and your long-term goals.

Timeline expectations:

- About two to four weeks from first call to offer.
- Assignments usually come with a short deadline, so plan time accordingly.

## How to Prepare

**Know the products:**

1. Solve problems on InterviewBit and read Scaler Topics articles.
2. Attend a free Scaler masterclass and note the funnel: registration, reminders, session, counselling call invitation.
3. Read about Scaler School of Technology and the 2026 program relaunches.
4. Follow founders' posts on LinkedIn for strategic signals.

**Practise ed-tech cases:**

- Design: an AI mock interviewer, a parent experience for SST.
- Improvement: InterviewBit retention, mentorship sessions.
- Metrics: Scaler Topics, the Modern Software & AI Engineering program.
- Root cause: masterclass conversion drops, assignment completion drops.
- Strategy and trade-offs: AI's impact on tech careers, live versus recorded classes, pricing models.

**Tailor your CV:** Highlight learner or user outcomes, experiments and cross-functional work. If you have taught, mentored or built learning tools, include it.

## FAQs

### Does Scaler hire freshers as product managers?

Scaler hires product interns and occasionally APMs, and Scaler School of Technology students may have internal opportunities. Most PM hires have a few years of experience, often from engineering, analytics or consulting.

### Is there a take-home assignment in Scaler's PM interview?

Candidates have reported an assignment as part of the PM process. Expect a problem tied to a Scaler product, followed by a discussion where interviewers probe your assumptions and metrics.

### What kind of product questions does Scaler ask?

Expect ed-tech-specific cases: improving engagement and completion, designing AI learning features, measuring outcome-focused programs, and diagnosing funnel drops from free learners to paid programs.

### How important is technical knowledge for Scaler PMs?

Because Scaler's users are engineers and aspiring engineers, being comfortable with coding concepts helps you empathise with learners and work with engineering teams. You are not expected to code in interviews, but you should understand the learning journey of a developer.

### How is Scaler changing because of AI?

Scaler has rebuilt its flagship programs around AI, launched AI-focused programs and positioned itself as AI-native. PM candidates should be ready to discuss how AI changes both what Scaler teaches and how it teaches.
