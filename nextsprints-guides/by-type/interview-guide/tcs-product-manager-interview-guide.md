## Introduction

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is India's largest IT services company, with roughly US$30 billion in annual revenue and around 6 lakh employees. Most students know TCS as a mass recruiter of engineers through the National Qualifier Test (NQT). Fewer know that TCS also runs a substantial products and platforms business, with software used by banks, insurers, retailers, governments and universities around the world. That is where most product manager and product owner roles sit.

Product roles at TCS fall into two broad groups. The first is the **Products and Platforms** units: TCS BaNCS (core banking, capital markets and insurance), ignio (AIOps from TCS subsidiary Digitate), TCS iON (assessments, exams and learning), TCS MasterCraft (including TransformPlus for legacy modernisation), TCS Optumera (AI retail merchandising) and TCS WisdomNext (GenAI orchestration, launched in 2024). The second is **client-facing digital product roles**, where TCS staff act as Product Owners on clients' agile programmes, for example owning a bank's mobile app backlog or a retailer's e-commerce journey.

In 2026, TCS is repositioning itself around AI. It reported AI services revenue of about US$2.3 billion annualised in FY26, launched a "Human+AI" operating model, and set up HyperVault, an AI data-centre subsidiary. For PM candidates, that means more product roles around AI platforms and AI features inside existing products.

> What makes TCS different
> Candidates report that TCS product interviews care less about consumer "product sense" puzzles and more about domain depth, stakeholder management and delivery discipline. A PM on TCS BaNCS who understands how a bank posts an interest accrual is more valuable than one who can design a fancy app screen.

## PM Role

> TCS Product Manager
> A TCS Product Manager or Product Owner defines and delivers software products or product backlogs, either for TCS's own platforms (such as BaNCS, ignio or iON) or for clients' digital products, balancing customer needs, domain rules, delivery constraints and commercial goals.

Responsibilities:

- Own the roadmap and backlog for a product module (for example, BaNCS deposits or iON assessment engine)
- Translate client and market requirements into epics, user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work with architects, engineering, QA and implementation teams across India and onshore locations
- Support pre-sales: demos, RFP responses and solution walkthroughs for banks, retailers or governments
- Track adoption, defects and client satisfaction after releases
- Ensure compliance with domain regulations (banking, data protection, exam integrity)

Where PMs sit:

| Unit | Product focus | Typical PM work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TCS BaNCS | Core banking, payments, capital markets, insurance | Domain-heavy features, regulatory changes, cloud-native modules |
| ignio (Digitate) | AIOps, autonomous IT and business operations | Automation content, integrations, AI features |
| TCS iON | Exams, NQT, learning, campus software | High-scale B2G and B2C flows, candidate experience |
| TCS MasterCraft | TransformPlus, DataPlus, DevOps tools | Developer tools, GenAI-assisted modernisation |
| TCS Optumera | Retail merchandising AI | Forecasting, pricing, assortment UX |
| Client digital programmes | Banking apps, retail platforms, insurance portals | Product Owner on agile teams |

Locations: Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Gandhinagar and Delhi NCR, with onshore roles in the UK, US, Europe and the Middle East.

## Job Requirements

Qualifications:

- Engineering, MBA or domain degrees (finance, commerce, retail) are common
- Lateral PM roles typically need 4–10 years of experience, often including business analysis or domain consulting
- Domain certifications help: banking (for example, IIBF courses), agile (CSPO, SAFe POPM), cloud fundamentals

Skills:

| Skill | Why it matters at TCS |
| --- | --- |
| Domain knowledge | BaNCS, Optumera and iON serve regulated, complex industries |
| Requirements and backlog management | Most work runs in agile or SAFe programmes |
| Stakeholder management | Clients, delivery leaders and onshore teams all have a say |
| Data and analytics | Adoption, defects and KPIs drive roadmap decisions |
| Communication | Pre-sales demos and client workshops are frequent |
| AI literacy | WisdomNext, ignio and AI features are now part of most roadmaps |

Experience level guide:

- **Campus (0–2 years):** Business analyst or associate product roles; learning the domain is expected
- **Product Owner / PM (4–9 years):** Owns a module or a client product backlog
- **Senior PM (8–13 years):** Owns a product line, influences pre-sales and roadmap
- **Product Head (12+ years):** Owns a product P&L and multiple teams

## Interview Process Breakdown

Lateral process as reported by candidates on AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and Prepfully:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Application or Referral] --> B[Online Assessment or Screening Call]
    B --> C[Technical and Product Round]
    C --> D[Managerial Round]
    D --> E[HR Round]
    E --> F[Offer]
```

| Stage | Format | Duration | Focus areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screening | Phone or iON-based test | 20–60 mins | Background, aptitude or domain basics |
| Technical and product | Video or in person | 45–60 mins | Product lifecycle, go-to-market, agile, domain case |
| Managerial | Video or in person | 30–60 mins | Stakeholder scenarios, client handling, prioritisation |
| HR | Phone or video | 20–30 mins | Compensation, notice period, relocation |

Detailed breakdown:

- Technical and product round:

Candidates report questions on the product lifecycle, writing user stories, defining MVPs and go-to-market plans. Domain teams add specifics: a BaNCS round may ask how a payment flows from UPI to the core ledger, while an iON round may ask how to scale an exam to lakhs of candidates.

- Managerial round:

Expect scenarios such as "A client wants a feature that conflicts with the product roadmap. What do you do?" or "Your release has a critical defect two days before go-live." Interviewers typically look for calm prioritisation and transparent communication.

- Product case:

Some teams ask a short case: design a feature, improve a metric or analyse a drop. Practising with TCS products (for example, improving NQT, measuring ignio success) gives you relevant examples.

- HR round:

Standard discussion of CTC, variable pay, location and joining date. TCS HR policies are structured, so negotiation room depends on level.

Campus process: graduates enter through TCS NQT and are assigned to Ninja, Digital or Prime tracks based on scores and interviews (technical, managerial, HR). MBA hiring at partner campuses includes a resume shortlist, sometimes a group discussion or case, and interviews for business analyst, domain consultant or product roles. Moving into product ownership usually happens after one to three years.

Timeline expectations:

- Lateral: 2–5 weeks
- Campus: results within days of interviews; joining dates can vary

## How to Prepare

Preparation timeline:

1. **Weeks 1–2: Learn the domain.** Pick the unit you are targeting. For BaNCS, learn core banking basics: accounts, ledgers, interest, payments (UPI, NEFT, RTGS, SWIFT). For iON, learn how computer-based exams work. For ignio, read about AIOps and ITSM.
2. **Weeks 2–3: Practise product cases.** Design, improvement, metrics and RCA questions using TCS products.
3. **Week 3: Agile and delivery.** Be ready to write user stories, explain Scrum ceremonies and handle scope changes.
4. **Week 4: Behavioural stories.** Prepare stories on stakeholder conflict, delivery under pressure and influencing without authority.

Practice resources:

- TCS product pages and press releases (BaNCS, ignio, iON, WisdomNext)
- TCS annual reports and earnings call commentary for strategy context
- Gartner and analyst summaries on core banking and AIOps
- NextSprints TCS question bank

> Success tip
> Tie every answer back to client outcomes: fewer outages, faster product launches, lower costs or better exam reliability. TCS sells outcomes to enterprises and governments, and interviewers listen for that framing.

## FAQs

### Does TCS have a formal APM program?

TCS does not run a branded APM programme like some product companies. Graduates usually join through NQT or MBA campus hiring as engineers, business analysts or domain consultants, and move into product owner roles within one to three years. Units such as TCS iON, BaNCS and Digitate also hire associate-level product staff laterally.

### How technical are TCS product interviews?

Moderately technical. You are not expected to code, but you should understand APIs, integration, databases and cloud basics, especially for BaNCS and ignio roles. Candidates report questions on how systems interact and how they would handle defects or performance issues.

### Is domain knowledge mandatory?

For BaNCS and Optumera roles, strong domain knowledge is a major advantage and sometimes required. For iON and client digital programmes, general product skills plus a willingness to learn the domain can be enough at junior levels.

### How long does the TCS PM interview process take?

Lateral candidates typically report 2–5 weeks from first call to offer. Delays usually come from background verification and offer approvals rather than interviews.

### Can I move from a TCS engineering role into product management?

Yes. Internal mobility is common. Engineers often move to business analyst or product owner roles in client programmes, then into product units. Building domain expertise and taking ownership of requirements are the usual stepping stones.
